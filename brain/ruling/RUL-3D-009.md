Harry, 20 Nov 2026, after the realistic first-person hand (tools/phone3d/hand_phone_gta.py).
Keep the game's toon style. "Realistic" is a quality bar inside it:
- correct body proportions everywhere (heads, hands, limbs, scale against props, goals, doors);
- no visual bugs (body parts through each other, floating or sinking feet, popping);
- pitches and sponsor boards at the right scale and placement, clean textures;
- good camera views and framing;
- hands: proper finger poses, really holding things;
- picking things up and interacting with props: real contact, no gaps, no clipping.
The realistic hand is the bar for hand POSE and CONTACT quality, not a reason to switch models.
