Replaces RUL-UI-001. Harry, 1 Nov 2026: "on RUL-UI-001, toggles for skins are okay."

- **Skins may be toggles.** A skin restyles the whole UI (buttons, cards, bars,
  background) over the same layout and rules. It can sit in Settings → UI as a
  player choice. First one: Glass stadium, the default (1 Nov 2026).
- **Everything else is unchanged from RUL-UI-001.** A gameplay change or a new
  look for one feature is built as THE game, with no "New | Old" row. Old
  versions live in git. Anything still being judged goes on a test/dev page.
