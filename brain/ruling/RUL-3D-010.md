Every generated person (managers, staff, players, fans): hair texture, hairstyle, beard and
features follow the person's heritage, never picked independently of skin. Afro-textured
styles (fades, waves, short afro, high-top, twists, locs, cornrows, curly tops for mixed
race) on fitting people; straight styles like a quiff never on coily-hair presets. Real
variety within each heritage, no stereotypes. Reuse the player's hair system, which
already gets this right. A test enforces it (tests/star/peoplePresets.mts).
