- **young**: And number one is Kaiki, from Como.
- **pick**: He's twenty-three, and worth fifteen million euros. A quarter of Hall's price.
- **compare**: Hall against Kaiki, on what makes Hall special: dribbles, ball recoveries, blocked shots and long balls.
- **edge**: And Kaiki creates more chances, and dribbles even more. Early days though: only five games' worth of minutes.
