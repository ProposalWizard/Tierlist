# v07 · Who replaces Lewis Hall at Newcastle?

Format from a replacement-finder TikTok Mikey shared (format only; our own words, data and look). Twin method v2 on 140 left-backs in the top 5 leagues (2025/26 + 2026/27, best season each), then 23-and-under only. Ages, values and clubs: FotMob player pages, 9 Nov 2026. Hall's move to Man United is Mikey's premise, not checked. 51 s, Hugo.

- **hook**: Lewis Hall is off to Manchester United. So who replaces him at Newcastle?
- **profile**: He's second of every Premier League full-back for dribbles, blocked shots and ball recoveries. But not many assists.
- **method**: So we compared him with all 140 left-backs in Europe's top five leagues. Every stat per ninety, two thousand tests.
- **map**: The two closest: Javi Galán and Neco Williams.
- **judge**: But Galán is thirty-one. And Williams is twenty-five, worth forty-two million euros.
- **filter**: Hall is only twenty-two. So: twenty-three and under only.
- **young**: And number one is Kaiki, from Como.
- **pick**: He's twenty-three, and worth fifteen million euros. A quarter of Hall's price.
- **compare**: Hall against Kaiki, on what makes Hall special: dribbles, blocked shots, ball recoveries and passes.
- **edge**: And Kaiki creates more chances, and dribbles even more. Early days though: only five games' worth this season.
- **end**: So Newcastle's next Lewis Hall? Kaiki. Would you sign him? Follow for more.
