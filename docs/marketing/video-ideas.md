# Video ideas and reference TikToks (Mikey, 3 Nov 2026)

The one list of video ideas for Knowitball, and the TikToks they come from.
Add new references and ideas here, newest at the bottom of each section.
Reference videos themselves are never kept in the repo: each one's frames
and transcript were made with `scripts/screen-video/` into the git-ignored
`.recordings/` folder, which is lost when a cloud session ends. Fetch again
with the link if needed.

Two ways to use a reference:
- **Animated, same story:** keep their story and facts, redraw it in our
  cartoon style (the Cruyff video), with our narrator and voice.
- **New situation:** keep their format (the shape, the hook, the reveal) and
  point it at a new player, match or "you" from Road to Ballon d'Or.

Numbers in any idea must be checked before a video is made; theirs are only a
starting point.

## Ideas

### From the earlier list (2 Nov 2026)

1. **Who would win? (series).** More finals in the R2 shape, e.g. Arsenal + you v PSG, Chelsea + you v Barcelona.
2. **Replace the striker.** "We swapped [club]'s striker for a sixth-tier nobody. Here's their season."
3. **Ballon d'Or power ranking.** Monthly, or by league; "#1 is you". (Made once: the 2027 top 10.)
4. **Best XI in the world, plus you.** The XI forms on a team sheet; you take the last spot.
5. **Rate my career.** "Non-league to Real Madrid in 5 seasons. Realistic?" Real data from the game.
6. **Every club that wanted me.** The transfer window as a reveal, smallest to biggest club.
7. **Golden Boot race.** The real top scorers' chart; "you" overtake them in the last week.
8. **Draft your dream strike partner.** A tier list of strikers to play next to you.
9. **Ballon d'Or night recap.** Every winner aged 41 down to 21, and where you'd fit. (Made.)
10. **Wrong-answers-only scout report.** Every top club's scout on "you" ("left foot: decorative").

### From the new references (3 Nov 2026)

**Animated, same story** (our cartoon style, the Cruyff template):

11. **Koeman, the biggest robbery (R8).** As it is: Koeman's 1987-88 season told by our narrator, ending on the year's real winner. Ready to make now.
12. **Milito's treble, forgotten (R7).** As it is: the three finals he scored in, then where he actually finished.
13. **Messi 2021, a robbery? (R5).** As it is, the case against: club season, top scorers, the Copa América goals by round. End on "which robbery next?".
14. **Ronaldo 2008 v Messi 2012 (R10).** As it is: numbers v nights that mattered, the two Chelsea penalties side by side.
15. **Ballon d'Or robberies (series, R5/R8/R7).** One a week: Koeman 1988, Milito 2010, Sneijder 2010, Ribéry 2013, Lewandowski 2020 (no award). Same open, same end card.

**New situation** (their format, new subject):

16. **The age-match before a big game (R12).** Before any big fixture, pick its star and line him up against the greats at the exact same age, then at the same minutes. Next ones: whoever is the young star of the next Clásico, Manchester derby or Champions League night. Road to Ballon d'Or version: "your player at 19 v Messi at 19".
17. **Could he do it on a cold, rainy night in Stoke? (R13).** The famous line, aimed at a new player each time (Yamal, Haaland in Spain, Kane in the snow), using real weather for every away game. Road to Ballon d'Or version: the weather in the game changes the ball.
18. **Three rules to the Ballon d'Or (R9).** Take this season's top 10 and knock them out by rules. Run it every month; also a Premier League version, a young-players version, and an all-time version ("only players who won the UCL, only..."). Road to Ballon d'Or version: the last name standing is "you".
19. **What if the nations were switched? (R6).** Swap two players' countries and work out what each would have won: Haaland for Argentina, Kane for France, Salah for Spain. The odds come from real tournament records.
20. **Ridiculous opponents (R11).** "Could [club] win against...": 10 blind footballers, 11 goalkeepers, 50 clones (R1), a team of 60-year-olds, 11 Haalands. Animated in our style, told play by play. Road to Ballon d'Or version: "you" against 11 of yourself.
21. **The best season ever, settled (R10).** Two great seasons, numbers v trophies, with one twist moment both share. E.g. Haaland 2022-23 v Salah 2017-18, Kane 2025-26 v Lewandowski 2020-21.
22. **The robbery that did not happen (R5 turned round).** A season that looks like a robbery but was fair, proved with numbers. Good for comments.
