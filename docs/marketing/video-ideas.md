# Video ideas and reference TikToks (Mikey, 3 Nov 2026)

The one list of video ideas for Knowitball, and the TikToks they come from.
Add new references and ideas here, newest at the bottom of each section.
Reference videos themselves are never kept in the repo: each one's frames
and transcript were made with `scripts/screen-video/` into the git-ignored
`.recordings/` folder, which is lost when a cloud session ends. Fetch again
with the link if needed.

Two ways to use a reference:
- **Animated, same story:** keep their story and facts, redraw it in our
  cartoon style (the Cruyff video), with our narrator and voice.
- **New situation:** keep their format (the shape, the hook, the reveal) and
  point it at a new player, match or "you" from Road to Ballon d'Or.

Numbers in any idea must be checked before a video is made; theirs are only a
starting point.
