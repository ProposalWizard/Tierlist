# 3D world research: driving, hair growth and barber, every 3D area (20 Nov 2026)

Research only (Opus builder), no game code changed. Asked by Harry (docs/plans/LIVING_WORLD_IDEAS.md).
Builds on docs/research/stylised-bodies-2026-11-20.md. Stills and measurements were in the session
scratchpad (world-research/perf/). [measured] = measured with scripts/perf3d/proof.mjs on a software-GPU
build machine (trust counts, not ms); [source] = web page; [reasoned] = our conclusion.

## A. Driving, house to training / matchday
- Recommended: our own simple car that follows a drawn road line (no physics library): lane steer,
  throttle/brake, faked roll/pitch, wheels turned by distance. Deterministic, can't flip, cheap on phones,
  filmable with `__frameStep`. 30 s to training, 45 s to a match, Skip from frame 1, a Setting
  "Drive to training: Always / Matchdays / Never". Calls 8-15 s in through the 3D phone on the dash.
- Blocker: the six Higgsfield shop cars (`shop3d/items/car-*-hf.glb`, ~23k tris) are one fused mesh with
  wheels baked in. Fix: a Blender script (`tools/drive3d/`) that cuts out 4 wheels and fills the arches
  (~1 day + an eye check per car); fallback: one shared toon wheel over the baked ones.
- Roads: Kenney City Kit Roads / Suburban / Commercial / Car Kit (CC0), Quaternius props (CC0), one road
  line with kerbs and seeded scatter, InstancedMesh for lamps and trees. No real brands.
- Not needed: cannon-es RaycastVehicle (MIT), Rapier vehicle controller (Apache-2.0, ~15 MB), ecctrl (R3F only).
- How others do it [source/memory]: GTA (calls while driving), Mafia (story in the car), FIFA The Journey
  (cars only in cut scenes), FM/FC career (no travel).

## B. Hair growth, bathroom, barber (Red Dead Redemption 2 rules)
- RDR2 [source]: hair and beard grow in stages, one stage at a time behind a fade; only shorter cuts;
  barber does what you can't; pomade wears off (~5 days barber, 2 self); tonic speeds growth.
- Ours: `hairLen` and `beardLen` 0-6 on the career; hair +1 about every 3 weeks, beard +1 about weekly,
  applied on the week tick. Styles need a minimum length (crew 1, curly top 2, twists 3, man bun 4, locs and
  braids 4+). Texture follows heritage (RUL-3D-010).
- Build: short end in the toon shader (`uHairCut` already shaves and paints stubble; make stubble a 0-1 ramp);
  longer lengths = 2-4 modelled versions per style from `tools/hair3d/build_hair.py` (about 30 → 70 files,
  runtime cost unchanged). Beards: 0 clean, 1-2 shader stubble, 3-6 modelled beard caps. Morph targets don't fit.
- Bathroom: a new `TIER_ROOMS` room reusing the dressing-room mirror (Trim / Shave / Style / Products).
  Barber: chair, mirror, bench as smart objects; 6-8 s cut; prices £20 local up to £500 celebrity barber at
  the villa. New clips via `tools/mocap3d/retarget.py`.

## Shared "world kit" (`lib/star/world3d/`) [reasoned]
- Sims idea [source: Northwestern "Programming Objects in The Sims"]: dumb people, smart objects that
  advertise what they offer and carry the animation script. RDR2 idea [source]: daily schedules.
- `smartObject.ts` (seat, lean, counter, mirror, door, tv, fridge, gym, barberChair, car…), `routine.ts`
  (DayClock from the career calendar, seeded routines, test `tests/star/routine.mts`), `npc.ts`
  (Person3D + `TapWalker` + `GaitBlend`, books an object slot, memory hook), `interact.ts` (tap → walk up →
  card), `budget.ts` (NPC caps per tier in `TIER_PROFILES`).
- Path finding: our `lib/star/tapWalk.ts` first; three-pathfinding (MIT) if outgrown; recast-navigation-js
  (MIT, ~2 MB WASM) only for the PC training ground; three-mesh-bvh (MIT) for capsule-vs-walls.
- Scale: phone ≤4 moving NPCs on screen, ≤8 present; PC 10-20. Far mixers every 2nd-4th frame, freeze
  off-screen, impostors for far seated people.

## Training centre and food hall
Car park (drive ends here) → reception → changing room → gym → canteen → physio → pitch. Phone: one room at a
time (house `roomBuild` pattern); PC: open plan. 6-10 squad mates on routines (arrive 08:30, gym/physio,
canteen 12:30, train 14:00, leave 17:00; personality nudges it). Tap → chat / form / banter / invite (racing,
drive, casino) / lend money; relationship moves and he remembers (ties to the living-world event design).
