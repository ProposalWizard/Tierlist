# Mikey's standing rules, 25 Oct 2026

1. Permission prompts last. On a big task, do every step that needs no approval first, and leave anything that may ask Mikey to tap Allow (an artifact publish, a connector call) until the end, so the work never sits waiting while he is away. Ask for permission as little as possible.
2. Discuss means discuss. When Mikey asks to measure, report or talk about something, report back and do not change the game. Build only what he asks to be built ("I just asked you to discuss it").
