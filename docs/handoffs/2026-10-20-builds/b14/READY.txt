B14: running after a chance (20 Oct 2026)

What it does
- Any pass you play in open play with a goal in the picture now plays on in the same picture,
  whatever the Chances setting (was: Chances: New + a build-up only). Not on shots, corners,
  free kicks or penalties.
- Phone, and whenever you don't steer: Auto run takes you into the best space it can see,
  onside, toward goal, sprints when it is far, and sprints onto a ball played back to you.
- Computer: W A S D (or the arrows) steer you, Shift sprints. Let go: Auto run takes over again
  after 0.6 s. The keys only belong to the match while your run is live (they never touch the aim drag).
- "After you pass: Joystick" (Harry changes) keeps its thumb stick, now with the same legs and bar.
- The man who takes your pass shoots, or plays it to your feet, or slides you in, by how much
  better your spot is than his and how good a passer he is. No captaincy needed. Captains still
  get "Call for it".
- Stamina bar top-left of the pitch (phone and computer), shown in open-play chances.

Stamina = energy (one system)
- The bar is the 3D games' sprint bar (6 s of sprinting full to empty at physical 50; refills
  when you ease off). It can never refill past your match energy: energy 60 -> the bar stops at
  60%; the part past that is shown hatched.
- Sprinting costs real match energy: 0.12 a second (a full 6 s sprint = 0.7). It comes off the
  same energy the career saves. Measured: Auto run sprints ~0.7 s a match (0.08 energy); a
  player steering into the box ~1.7 s (0.20 energy).
- Speeds come from your pace (pace 50: run 5.1, sprint 6.9 m/s; pace 90: sprint 7.9).

Measured (scripts/b14-measure.mts, 500 matches, a bot that passes 35% of open-play chances)
                                 before   after (Auto run)  after (steering into the box)
  chances handed to you a match   5.82     5.82              5.81
  your goals                      0.98     1.04              1.06
  team-mate goals off your pass   0.39     0.32              0.34
  team goals with you involved    1.37     1.40              1.39
  their goals                     1.16     1.14              1.16
  ball back to you, same picture  0.00     0.15              0.20
- Ball back by passer (tests/star/runAfter.mts, 160 chances x 5 kinds): passing 45 -> 9.1% of
  his reads, passing 88 -> 15.4%. Standing still 4.3%, Auto run 13.6%, sprinting into the box
  30.6%. You reach 85% of the balls played back. Onside when you passed: offside when he looks
  up 2.5%.
