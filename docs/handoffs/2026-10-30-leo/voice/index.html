Line by line

What changed

The old voice read every line like a news reader. The new one is a different kind of speech model with an emotion control: it rises on a goal, holds the big word, and the co-commentator stays calm and low. Each line was recorded up to five times. A speech checker kept the take with every word right and the right amount of excitement. Then each line got a TV commentary-box sound: microphone tone, compression and a little room.

Honest limits: it is still a computer voice. Some calls may sound a bit strained at the top. Tell us which lines sound wrong and they can be remade one by one, or replaced on the Sound Board.
