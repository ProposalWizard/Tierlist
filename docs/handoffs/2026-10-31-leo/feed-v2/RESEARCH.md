## Research (what was searched, what was found)

- Searched: TikTok football edit captions ("rate this goal", POV), football
  Twitter slang (ratio, cooked, aura, ball knowledge), transfer reliability tiers.
- Found little that is current or reliable: tool guides (CapCut) say edits are
  beat-cut highlights with big on-screen captions and POV hooks; slang glossaries
  confirm "ratio" (more replies than likes, a pile-on), "cooked", "aura" (grew on
  football and basketball X); fan-made tier lists rank news from tier 1
  (rarely wrong) to tier 5 (aggregators).
- The rest is **reasoned** from knowledge of how football X and TikTok read in
  2024–26, not measured from live accounts.
