Transcript used: medium.en with no silence-skipping (`transcript_medium_noVAD.txt`), cross-checked with the first base.en pass (`transcript.txt`). All 14 contact sheets were opened, plus full-resolution frames at every point below.
Pictures: `keyframes/` (cropped to the game column, each under 300 KB, file names listed in the table below).
Nothing here has been checked against the game's code. Every line is what Harry said or what was on his screen.

How he works in this video: he is not testing a patch, he is talking a redesign through while clicking around his own career (Chelsea, "hb", age 16, Overall 40, star rating 2.4, 8k money, next match Coventry v Chelsea, Premier League week 3). Legend. **TESTED** = he was looking at / clicking the live game screen while saying it. **SPOKEN** = a design idea or rule about something that is not on screen at that moment (or is not in the game yet). Tags: [BUG] [CHANGE] [LIKE] [IDEA] [QUESTION].
Uncertain words are flagged as "UNCERTAIN" with what each transcript heard.
