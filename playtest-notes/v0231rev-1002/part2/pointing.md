# Pointing moments: where the mouse was when Harry said "here", "this", "there" (v0231rev-1002, part 2)

Harry points with the mouse while he talks. This file lines the cursor up with the words, using the original video ("Patch notes and trial review part 2.mp4", 13:45, 1280x720).

How it was done: word-level timestamps from medium.en with no silence skipping (`transcript.txt` has the sentences; the word timings were kept off-repo). About 110 "this / that / here / there / these / those" words were in the transcript. **87 moments** were chosen where the word points at something (the rest are "that works", "that does look good" and similar). For each, the cursor was searched on every frame from 0.8 s before to 0.5 s after the word (every third frame). Two shapes were matched: the white arrow with a black middle, and the white pointing hand that shows over links and video buttons. Each match was then checked by eye on a crop. A few were fixed by hand (01:21, 01:53, 06:44 and 10:23 on the frame; 02:18 and 08:33 from a frame a little before or after the word). Positions are good to about 10 pixels. Where the cursor was hidden or not found, the row says so and has no picture.

Confidence: **CLEAR** = cursor rests on one named element and the words and the screen name it. **LIKELY** = cursor on or near the area, and the meaning follows from cursor plus words, or from the words and the one candidate on screen. **UNCLEAR** = cursor not on anything useful, or not found. A magenta ring and arrow mark the cursor in each picture. The browser bar and the page header are cropped or covered.
