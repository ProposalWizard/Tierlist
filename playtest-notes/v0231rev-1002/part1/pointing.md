# Pointing moments: where the mouse was when Harry said "this", "here" or "there" (v0231rev-1002 part 1)

Harry points with the mouse while he talks. This file lines the cursor up with the words, using the original video.

How it was done: word-level times came from medium.en with no silence skipping. The words "this", "here", "there", "these" and "that" were listed with their times (about 90 of them). 41 moments were kept where the word points at something on screen. The others are "that is good", "this is a review" and similar. For each kept moment the frames from 0.8 s before to 0.5 s after the word were compared. The cursor shows as a dark arrow with a white edge (or a magnifier over a picture, a hand over a clip). It was found by the change between frames and then checked by eye on a crop. Positions are good to about 15 pixels. Where the cursor was not found, the row says so and has no picture.

Confidence: **CLEAR** = the cursor rests on one named thing and the words name it. **LIKELY** = the cursor is on or near the thing and the meaning follows from cursor and words. **UNCLEAR** = the cursor is on nothing useful, or is not found.

Totals: **41 pointing moments**, **8 CLEAR**, **20 LIKELY**, **13 UNCLEAR**. **37** have an annotated picture in `keyframes/` (`ptr-MMSS-<short>.jpg`: a magenta ring and arrow on the cursor). The browser bars and the chat pane are cropped out of every picture. Four rows are about a messaging window; they have no picture because the window is personal.
