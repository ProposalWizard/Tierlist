## Summary

### v0.20 (Mikey v0.7)
**Liked**
- The star-rating section of the notes: "I like this whole section I think this is fine" (p2); the gate ladder idea "that makes sense" (p4).
- Training screens in the Home-screen style, "really cool now" (p9). Home screen tidy-up looks good (p11).
- Contract showing real terms "is really cool" (p14, p25). Spin-your-player page "is cool for the homepage" (p15).
- Five-a-side: "I'm liking the five a side", his team won 5-0; opposition long shot shown "that's good to see" (p17, p21). Tap-to-pass from five-a-side, "I kind of like that feature" (p29).
- Boots cut to 7: "These are cool" (p6). The three "not seen on a screen yet" items do show up (p35). Star Pass "would be really cool" (p43).

**Found broken / wrong**
- Star points scale is far too small: one National League game took him 1.0 to 1.8; on camera one Prem draw (+68 points, 17 x 4) took him 1.0 to 2.1 (p1, p33).
- Popup says "2 STARS Non-league regular" while he is in the Premier League (p34).
- Spin-your-player: the back view "does not work" (a flat stand-in; the page itself says there is no back view yet) (p15).
- Five-a-side: his player's skin tone looked different from the one he picked once the game started (p20). Opposition long shots are always blocked, scoreline 5-0 "maybe too easy" (p22).
- Home screen at age 16: "the 2D flick doesn't work here" (p26).
- Real match (not a patch-note item): a defender intercepts a drag shot "even though he's on the wrong side of me"; an assist was credited to him "even though I wasn't the one who actually assisted him" (p30, p32).
- Lifestyle/Style page is "just really bad": too long, fame per item not visible, level names meaningless, no pictures (p37-p40). Shop page layout hides the four tiles behind Store and Casino (p36).

**Wants changed**
- Multiply the star-points scale "massively" (his numbers: 1.1 to 2.0 about 100,000 points; 8.1 to 9.0 about 10 million); gates by league, World Cup about 4 appearances (p3, p4). Make star rating mean something, or drive a Star Pass with unlocks (p5).
- Style: level 5 items should unlock something big; regroup as a Holiday section (p7).
- Training: after 2 sessions say "no more sessions this week", not "1 of 2 remaining" (p8). Dribbling drill "still pretty cooked", needs to look better (p10).
- Remove the 0-100 score card after each trial drill (p13, p19).
- Contract: replace the paper with a cutscene, generated player and manager signing (p14, p25). Remove/replace the newspaper page (p24).
- Stats tab order (p27). Shop hub: four tiles above Store and Casino (p36).
- Lifestyle: real names per level, pictures, hover detail, always show fame (p38-p40). Boots/shop as a walk-around gallery (p42).
- Tap-to-pass in the real match; foul more likely from behind, sometimes a penalty (p29, p31).

### v0.21 (Mikey v0.8 - Sponsors Revamp)
