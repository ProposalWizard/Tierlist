# Pointing moments: where the mouse was when Harry said "here", "this", "there" (v023rev-1001)

Harry (and Mikey, who is on a call and speaks too) point with the mouse while they talk. This file lines the cursor up with the words, using the original video.

How it was done: word-level timestamps from medium.en with no silence skipping. About 480 "this / that / here / there / these" words were in the transcript; **87 moments** were chosen where the word really points at something (the rest are "that is cool", "that one", "this is" and similar). For each, the cursor was found on every frame from 0.8 s before to 0.5 s after the word, by matching the cursor's shape (a white arrow outline with a dark middle) on the 1280x720 frame, then checked by eye on a crop. Where the cursor was a hand (over a clip or link) or hidden, or a clip was playing under it, it is marked "Not found" and has no picture. Positions are good to about 10 pixels.

Confidence: **CLEAR** = cursor resting on one named element, or the words and the screen name it and nothing contradicts. **LIKELY** = cursor on or near the area, meaning follows from cursor plus words (or: cursor not found but the screen at that second shows only one candidate). **UNCLEAR** = cursor not on anything useful, or not found.
