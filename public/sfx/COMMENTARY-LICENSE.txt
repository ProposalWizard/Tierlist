The cl-*.mp3 (lead commentator) and cc-*.mp3 (co-commentator) files are the
goal-video commentary. They were made offline with Kokoro
(https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro, Apache-2.0; ONNX build
https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx, MIT), voices "bm_george" (lead)
and "bm_fable" (co-commentator), by scripts/commentary/generate.py from the
lines in lib/star/goalClip/commentary.ts. Any of them can be replaced on the
Sound Board (/admin/sound-board).

The NEW voice (Leo's update switch, "New commentary voice") is a separate set
in public/sfx/v2/ (Chatterbox, MIT; see public/sfx/v2/LICENSE.txt and
scripts/commentary/README.md). These files stay as they are.
