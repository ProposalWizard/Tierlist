The cl-*.mp3 (lead commentator) and cc-*.mp3 (co-commentator) files in this
folder are the NEW goal-video commentary voice (Leo's update switch, "New
commentary voice"). Made offline on 30 Oct 2026 by
scripts/commentary/generate_v2.py from the lines in
lib/star/goalClip/commentary.ts.

- Speech model: Chatterbox, Resemble AI (https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox),
  MIT licence; ONNX export onnx-community/chatterbox-ONNX (MIT).
- Voice: an original voice. The reference recordings were made with Kokoro's
  bundled voices bm_lewis (lead) and bm_daniel (co-commentator)
  (https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro, Apache-2.0). No real person's voice was
  recorded, cloned or imitated.
- Commentary-box processing: scripts/commentary/broadcast.py (our own code).

Any line can be replaced on the Sound Board (/admin/sound-board); a
replacement plays in both voice sets.
