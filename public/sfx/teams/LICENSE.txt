The mp3 files in this folder are the COMMENTATOR TEAMS for goal videos and the
live late-goal call (Leo's update switch, "Different commentators"). Made
offline on 31 Oct 2026 by scripts/commentary/generate_teams.py from the lines
in lib/star/goalClip/commentators.ts (all lines are our own writing; none
quotes a real commentator).

- Speech model: Chatterbox, Resemble AI (https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox),
  MIT licence; ONNX export onnx-community/chatterbox-ONNX (MIT).
- Voices: seven ORIGINAL voices. Each reference recording was read by one of
  Kokoro's bundled synthetic voices (https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro,
  Apache-2.0; model files from github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx,
  release model-files-v1.0):
    The Anchor (lead)    bm_george
    The Poet (lead)      bf_emma
    The Deadpan (lead)   bf_isabella
    The Live Wire (lead) bm_fable
    The Old Pro (co)     bm_daniel
    The Analyst (co)     bf_lily
    The Mate (co)        am_puck
  No real person's voice was recorded, cloned or imitated, and no prompt
  names one.
- Word check: Whisper base.en (MIT) via sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0).
  Cleanness: DNSMOS (Microsoft DNS-Challenge, MIT).
- Commentary-box processing: scripts/commentary/broadcast.py (our own code).

Any line can be replaced on the Sound Board (/admin/sound-board) by its id.
