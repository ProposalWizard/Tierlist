3D SHOP TEST PAGE (/star-shop3d-dev) — where these files come from

Both files here were made by tools/shop3d/build_assets.py from two packs by
Quaternius (https://quaternius.com). Both packs are released under:

  CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) — Public Domain Dedication
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. Credit anyway: models and animations by @Quaternius.

character.glb (≈1.0 MB)
  - Body, eyes, eyebrows: "Superhero_Male_FullBody" from Universal Base
    Characters (Standard, free edition).
    https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
    The pack's own licence file (License_Standard.txt) says: "License: CC0 1.0
    Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication ... Models by @Quaternius".
  - Hair: "Hair_SimpleParted" from the same pack.
  - Textures shrunk to 1024/512 px JPEG; the roughness map and the hair and
    eye normal maps were dropped. Nothing else changed.

anims.glb (≈0.3 MB)
  - Clips Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop and Interact from Quaternius'
    Universal Animation Library (Standard, free edition), file
    "UAL1_Standard.glb" (sha256 d867292451e432b735e2a910c2db6640fbea97b205d85a2e8ffed26da87972cf),
    https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
    The copy used was fetched from a public GitHub mirror of the free pack
    (wataqsk/project_clara, assets/universal_animation_library/), because
    itch.io's download links can't be scripted. Its mannequin mesh is not
    included; only the four clips, with rotations kept and the pelvis
    height re-based onto the body above.

three.js itself is not in this folder or in the site's bundle: the page loads
three@0.169.0 (MIT) from cdn.jsdelivr.net when it opens.

items/car-family-hf.glb (9 Oct 2026, 3D look H only)
  The Family Car shown in the 3D shop. Made for this game: a picture of an
  unbranded hatchback generated with Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5), turned into
  a 3D model with Higgsfield's Tripo H3.1 image-to-3D, then packed by
  scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs. No logos, badges or plates.

items/boot-hf.glb, items/boot-classic-hf.glb, items/boot-speed-hf.glb (9 Oct 2026, 3D look H only)
  The football boots shown in the 3D shop. Made for this game: three pictures
  of unbranded boots (a knit boot, a classic leather boot, a laceless speed
  boot) generated with Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5), each turned into a 3D model
  with Higgsfield's Tripo H3.1 image-to-3D, simplified to about 22-30k
  triangles with textures at 1024 px, then packed by
  scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs. No logos, stripes or text.

items/car-hatch-hf.glb, items/car-suv-hf.glb, items/car-sports-hf.glb, items/car-classic-hf.glb, items/car-super-hf.glb (9 Oct 2026, 3D look H only)
  The other five cars shown in the 3D shop (Hatchback, Luxury SUV, Sports Car,
  Classic Car, Supercar). Made for this game: a picture of each unbranded car,
  painted in its shop colour, generated with Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5), turned
  into a 3D model with Higgsfield's Tripo H3.1 image-to-3D, simplified to about
  23k triangles with textures at 1024 px, then packed by
  scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs. No logos, badges or plates.

items/watch-smart-hf.glb, items/watch-gold-hf.glb, items/watch-rolex-hf.glb, items/jewel-silver-hf.glb, items/jewel-diamond-hf.glb (9 Oct 2026, 3D look H only)
  The counter's watches and jewellery (Smartwatch, Gold Watch, Diamond Rolex,
  Silver Chain, Diamond Necklace). Made for this game the same way (Higgsfield
