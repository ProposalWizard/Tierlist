player.glb, player-buzz.glb, player-long.glb, manager.glb, anims.glb
  The approved 3D characters (Oct 2026), generated with Higgsfield for

toon-c1.glb, toon-c2.glb, toon-c3.glb (+ toon-c1/2/3.webp, the Settings pictures)
  "Style A" bodies C1, C2 and C3 (9 Oct 2026). Generated with Higgsfield for
  Knowitball (image to 3D), rigged to the MakeHuman skeleton the game already
  uses (tools/modeltest, the model-test round), then scaled to 1.83 m and
  given a grey kit texture and a kit/skin/hair mask by
  scripts/people3d/build_toon_bodies.py. Packed by
  scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs. Invented people: no real player's
  likeness, no real kit or brand.
