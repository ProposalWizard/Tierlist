3D PEOPLE — where these files come from

player.glb, player-buzz.glb, player-long.glb, manager.glb, anims.glb
  The approved 3D characters (Oct 2026), generated with Higgsfield for
  Knowitball and made small by scripts/people3d/build_people3d.py.

toon-p1.glb .. toon-p6.glb, toon-mgr.glb, toon-mgr2.glb (+ toon-h1..h6.webp, the Settings pictures)
  "Style A" people (9 Oct 2026): six footballers and two managers in suits.
  Concept pictures made with Higgsfield's image model and turned into 3D
  with Higgsfield (Tripo H3.1 image to 3D) for Knowitball, rigged to the
  MakeHuman skeleton the game already uses (tools/modeltest/fit.py), then
  scaled to 1.83 m, fingers weighted, given a grey kit texture and a
  kit/skin/hair mask by scripts/people3d/build_toon_bodies.py. Packed by
  scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs. Sources: tools/modeltest/p1..p6, mgr,
  mgr2. Invented people: no real player's likeness, no real kit or brand.
  (The earlier C1-C3 bodies were replaced by these; their sources stay in
  tools/modeltest/c1..c3.)
