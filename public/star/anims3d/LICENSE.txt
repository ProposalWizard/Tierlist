public/star/anims3d/ — football and casino moves for the 3D people

football.glb, casino.glb, football-ual.glb, casino-ual.glb

Every clip in these files is hand-made for Knowitball (tools/anims3d/build.py
and clips.py: poses keyed in code and solved onto the skeletons). No motion
capture and no third-party animation is in them. They are ours.

They contain only bone turns and the hips' position for two skeletons that
come from free (CC0 1.0, public domain) packs, used unchanged:
  - the new 3D people (public/star/people3d, public/star/onebody), see
    public/star/shop3d/LICENSE.txt and tools/blender-footballer/LICENSE-Quaternius-UBC.txt;
  - the old shop/garden footballer: Quaternius "Universal Base Characters"
    (CC0 1.0), https://quaternius.com.
The old footballer's resting finger curl is copied from Quaternius
"Universal Animation Library" Idle_Loop (CC0 1.0), https://quaternius.com.
CC0 asks for no credit; this note is for our own records.
