public/star/anims3d/mocap.glb, mocap-ual.glb — real motion capture for the 3D people

SOURCE
  Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
  http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu
  "This dataset of motions is free for all uses." (home page)
  "The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or redistributed without
  permission." (FAQ, "How can I use this data?")
  Acknowledgement the database asks for: "The data used in this project was
  obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from
  NSF EIA-0196217."

WHAT IS IN THE FILES
  Only bone turns and the hips' position, retargeted from the captures onto
  our two skeletons by tools/mocap3d (CC0 skeletons: see LICENSE.txt here and
  public/star/shop3d/LICENSE.txt). No CMU file is shipped as-is. Each clip
  names its capture in the file (scene extras clips[name].source), e.g.
  "CMU 11_01" = subject 11, trial 1. A few clips are ADAPTED: the capture with
  a small hand-made change on top (the source says what was changed).
