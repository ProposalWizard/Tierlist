NEW 2D SHOP — STORE ART (Settings → Look → "Shop: New")

Every picture in this folder was generated on Higgsfield (the team's shared
account), 9 Oct 2026, with the Nano Banana 2 Lite image model, from text
prompts only. No reference images, no real brands, no logos. Used under the
account's Higgsfield plan terms for generated output.

Made: 17 generations (16 kept), 1 credit each = 17 credits. One more request
was refused by the service (no charge). Packed to small WebPs by
tools/shop2d/pack.py (crop, chroma-key the green sheets, turn the black-
backed effects into transparency, resize).

  hero.webp            store banner (the boutique at night)
  bg.webp              page texture (navy hex grid)
  panel.webp           the buy-sheet backdrop
  frame-1..5.webp      card frames by level: bronze, sapphire, ruby, amethyst, gold
  rank-1..5.webp       level emblems for the level ladder (one sheet, cut up)
  badge-gold/blue/green.webp   badge shapes (green = the blue tag recoloured)
  icon-*.webp          tab icons: cans, boots, style, drip, gadgets, cars, homes, holiday
  burst.webp           purchase burst
  fx-energy/curve/touch/power.webp   can and boot effect glows
  divider.webp         section divider
