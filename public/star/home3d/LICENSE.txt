3D home (lib/star/home3d, components/star/Home3D.tsx) — files in this folder

car-*-lod.glb, boot-*-lod.glb
  Light copies (about 4,000 triangles a car, 2,500 a boot, 512 px textures)
  of the shop's own Higgsfield-generated models in public/star/shop3d/items/
  (see public/star/shop3d/LICENSE.txt for how those were made: Higgsfield
  gpt_image_2_5 picture, then Tripo H3.1 image-to-3D). Made by
  tools/home3d/make_lods.mjs and packed by scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs.
  No new models were generated for the home and no Higgsfield credits were
  spent on it. Invented, unbranded designs: no real logos, badges or plates.

Everything else in the home (the room, wardrobe, rails, garments on the
rails, mirror, trophy cabinet and the trophies themselves, the window views)
is built in code (lib/star/home3d/scene.ts, textures.ts). The trophies are
stylised shapes, not copies of any real trophy. The body is the game's own
(public/star/people3d, public/star/human3d; see their LICENSE files).

bike-lod.glb, jet-lod.glb (Oct 2026): light copies of the shop's own motorbike and jet
(public/star/shop3d/items/bike-hf.glb, jet-hf.glb), made by tools/home3d/make_lods.mjs.
terrace-props.glb (Oct 2026): a few of the 3D garden's Kenney CC0 pieces (hedge, lantern,
potted plant, lounge chair, oak, bush, wooden fence) copied out of public/star/garden3d/props.glb
by tools/home3d/make_terrace_props.mjs (see public/star/garden3d/LICENSE.txt). CC0, no change.
