The drive (lib/star/drive3d, components/star/Drive3D.tsx) — files in this folder

car-<name>.glb, car-<name>-lod.glb  (hatch, family, suv, sports, classic, super)
  The shop's own Higgsfield-generated cars (public/star/shop3d/items/car-*-hf.glb; see
  public/star/shop3d/LICENSE.txt for how those were made) and the home's light copies
  (public/star/home3d/car-*-lod.glb), with the four wheels cut out so they spin and steer,
  and dark wheel-arch liners added: tools/cars/run_all.sh (unpack.mjs, then Blender
  cut_wheels.py), packed by scripts/perf3d/shrink-models.mjs. The Family Car was simplified
  from 36k to about 23k triangles on the way. No new models were generated and no
  Higgsfield credits were spent on the drive. Invented, unbranded designs.
