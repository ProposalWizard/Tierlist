# Pose images

Original generated footballer poses. Dropped here, they become the figure layer
of the transfer and award templates.

Rules for a pose that works:

- **Plain white technical kit** — no badge, sponsor, number or pattern. White is
  what lets one image tint into all twenty clubs; a coloured kit can only ever
  be that club.
- **Both hands inside the frame.** Every pose worth having is made of arms.
- **Identical framing across the set** — same crop point, same camera distance,
  same head size. Consistency between the poses matters far more than any one of
  them looking good on its own; a set shot at different scales reads as eight
  different people.
- **Head upright, facing camera.** The face is replaced at an anchor, and a
  tilted or profile head has nowhere to put one.
- **Flat, evenly lit background**, or transparent.

Anchors are set once per pose in the Media Lab (`/star-dev/media-lab`): drop the
image in, move the three sliders until the face sits right, copy the numbers.
