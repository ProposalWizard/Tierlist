LIVE 3D SIGNING SCENE (/star-3d-area-dev/signing) — where these files come from

people.glb and anims.glb were made by tools/signing3d/build_assets.py from two
packs by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), both released under:

  CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) — Public Domain Dedication
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. Credit anyway: models and animations by @Quaternius.

people.glb (about 1.0 MB)
  - Body, eyes, eyebrows: "Superhero_Male_FullBody" from Universal Base
    Characters (Standard, free edition), https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
    (the pack's own License_Standard.txt: CC0 1.0).
  - Hair: "Hair_SimpleParted", "Hair_Long", "Hair_Buzzed", "Hair_Beard" from
    the same pack.
  - The LIGHT skin texture (the page tints it to any skin tone), shrunk to
    1024 px JPEG; normal map 512 px; hair 512 px. Unused vertex data dropped.

anims.glb (about 0.27 MB)
  - Clips Sitting_Idle_Loop, Sitting_Talking_Loop, Sitting_Exit, Idle_Loop and
    Idle_Talking_Loop from Quaternius' Universal Animation Library (Standard,
    free edition), file "UAL1_Standard.glb" (the same copy public/star/shop3d
    was built from). Rotations only, plus the pelvis re-based onto the body.
    The reach, the pen grip, the writing, the head turns and the handshake are
    worked out live on top of these in lib/star/signing3dScene.ts.

aviators.glb (about 0.06 MB)
  - The gold aviator sunglasses from public/star/star-pass/3d/reward-glasses.glb
    (Mikey's Star Pass reward, made in Blender by tools/star-pass-art), cut out on
    their own so they can sit on this body's head.

room-golden-hour.webp is a generated picture, used as the view out of the window.

The clothes, the face picture, the accessories, the office, the desk, the chairs,
the pen and the contract are built in code (lib/star/signing3dRig.ts,
signing3dScene.ts, signing3dTextures.ts). three.js (MIT) is the site's own
"three" package, loaded only when this scene starts.
