The headline · v0.10

The guard: the game can't quietly split into copies again

ProblemThe trial, training and five-a-side felt slightly different from a real match: the keeper dived differently, kicks came out harder or softer, and five-a-side's arrow pointed a bit off.
WhySix screens had their own copy of the match. Each was right on the day it was made; the real match kept improving and the copies drifted (12 differences in the trial and training alone). Nothing stopped a new copy.
FixEvery screen now plays the real match through one door and adds its extras around it. An automatic check (the guard) reads the code before every deploy and stops the site going live if anyone adds a copy. 4 copies left, down from 6; the list can only shrink.

On long shots the defence sat on its own keeper

ProblemHarry reported the back line looked wrong on long shots. It sat 10.4-13.6m from goal, 3 to 6m deeper than the edge of the box, every time.
WhyThe rule places the line at a fixed share of the ball's distance, which keeps dropping deeper as the ball gets further out. Past the box, that stops being what a real defence does.
FixThe line now holds the edge of the box when the ball is outside it: 13.8-16.1m out. This made long shots easier to score (21.8% to 26.7%), not harder.
