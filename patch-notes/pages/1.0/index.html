The headline · v0.16

The drawing is the team: every player you drew plays

ProblemYou drew a cutback with 5 defenders; the game served it with 4. That happened in 77% of cutbacks, 79% of midfield passes and 59% of byline crosses. It also invented extra men (15 to 58% of chances, depending on kind) and moved the keeper off the spot you drew in every corner and every penalty.
WhyHand-written "always" rules had piled up on top of the drawings: the keeper is never on his line, pick some of the defenders, nudge the ball, re-stand you after a pass, move the free-kick team-mate. Each was written for one situation and then applied to every chance.
FixAll of the "always" rules are deleted. The picture you drew is the picture you get: the same men, the same keeper spot, the same ball. Extra team-mates you drew become real runners you can pass to (they stand still at set pieces), each with a real name and face.

ProblemWith the new chance generator switched on, far fewer chances went in: a cutback or a one-on-one converted only 9.8% of the time.

On long shots the defence sat on its own keeper

ProblemHarry reported the back line looked wrong on long shots. It sat 10.4-13.6m from goal, 3 to 6m deeper than the edge of the box, every time.
WhyThe rule places the line at a fixed share of the ball's distance, which keeps dropping deeper as the ball gets further out. Past the box, that stops being what a real defence does.
FixThe line now holds the edge of the box when the ball is outside it: 13.8-16.1m out. This made long shots easier to score (21.8% to 26.7%), not harder.
SCORINGfixedworked out, not played

A team-mate's rebound counted as your goal

ProblemYou shot, it came back off the keeper, a team-mate scored, and the goal went down as yours with your assist gone.
WhyThe game remembers 'you shot' and only cleared it on one of the two ways a team-mate can finish a loose ball. On the other way, his goal was credited to you. An earlier fix only covered the first way, which is why it kept happening.
FixOnce a team-mate strikes the ball it stops being your shot, whichever way he finished it.
