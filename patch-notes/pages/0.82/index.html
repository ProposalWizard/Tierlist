Point at the screen (Shift+X)B11 · v0.74seen
Problem / Why

Your ask (20 Oct): “point someone towards something on a page through a shortcut (like say shift + x …) and they see my note”.

Fix

Four steps:

  1. Press Shift+X (or the 👉 Point button in the chat bubble). A bar says “Drag a box over the part you mean”.
  2. Drag a box, write a note, pick who it is for (for example @Mikey and @Leo). Press Send.
  3. They open the link. The page scrolls to your box, outlines it and shows your note.
  4. The note stays until they press Got it. The chat shows “Not yet” under each name until then.
