CHANGED

Admins now land on the Team boardseenreasoned
Problem

Admin from the site’s top menu opened Site content, not the Team board.

Why

The Team board is the admin home now.

Fix

Admins now land on the Team board (/admin/team) from:

  • Admin in the site’s top menu (it was Site content)
  • Settings → Developer → Admin pages
  • The top link of the admin slide-out: “Admin & Dev · Team”
  • The KIB Admin app, which opens the new /admin/home: admins go to the Team board, testers to the Ballon d’Or home, everyone else to the site
  • The back arrow on any game’s home
  • The old “Admin” breadcrumbs
Testers keep their home. The site menu’s Admin link is reasoned, not seen: it needs a real admin sign-in.
The left menu puts Team firstseen
Problem

The Team pages were not at the top of the admin menu.

Why

Admins now land on Team, so it goes first.

Fix

Team is first, with a people icon. It is a solid bar while you are in the Team area. Inside it: Team board, Team chat, Ideas, My to-do and Patch notes. Then Games: Ballon d’Or, Website, Studio. Then Other: Videos. In a game: Team (not lit), the game’s home, then its groups. The folded menu and the phone menu also put Team first.

Patch notes moved into the Team areaseen
Problem

Patch notes sat outside the Team pages.

Why

The team’s pages should sit together.

Fix

Patch notes is now the last page inside Team, after My to-do. The top of the Team room is a toggle: Board | Chat | Ideas | To-do.

All four skins have the same layoutseen
Problem

The new menu had to work in every skin.

Why

The admin pages have four looks.

Fix

Broadcast, Bento, Ops room and Clean light all show the same layout.
