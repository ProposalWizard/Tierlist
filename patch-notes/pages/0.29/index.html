The headline: the cheat menus and test pages are locked

Any player could open the cheat menu and 18 test pages. Now only admins can.

Problem

Settings → Developer tools (add money, coins, max skills, switch club, skip ahead) sat behind a plain "Show" button. Any player could press it. Pages like the Play Area, the 3D test areas and the gallery opened for anyone who typed the address.

Why

They were built as test tools and nothing checked who was using them. Locally that was fine. On the live site it let anyone give themselves money and skills.

Fix

  • Cheat menu: Developer tools shows for admins only, in the New UI and the Old UI.
  • 18 test pages: each one now says "Admins only" on the live site, for everyone who is not an admin.
  • Local copies still show everything. A test copy and a local run treat everyone as an admin, so you will not see the lock there. This is on purpose.
How we know: reasoned from the code. Not seen on the live site as a normal player: a test copy cannot show the lock, because it counts everyone as an admin.

What this means for you

Normal players lose the cheat menu. Admin accounts keep it. Testers who used it on the live site lose it too, until tester access is built (see Next).

The Ballon d'Or is much harder to win

Problem

From a Championship start, a career won it in 19 of 20 simulated careers, the first time in season 3. Even a National League start won it in 9 of 20. A League Two player could win it in seasons 1 and 2.

Why

Any division could win it, and the best rivals in the world scored too few goals to stop a strong player.

Fix

  • Premier League only. You also need a major trophy that season: the Premier League, the Champions League, the World Cup or the Euros.
  • Championship: you can only win it as the top scorer on the list AND a World Cup winner that season.
  • League One and below are not shortlisted at all.
  • The best rivals (rated 86 or more) have superstar seasons. A 90-rated striker scores about 25 to 45 goals. A 93-rated striker scores about 30 to 55.

A Championship start now wins about 4 seasons after reaching the Premier League.

How we know: measured: 20 simulated careers from each start (not played by hand). It may be too hard once you play the matches yourself. It is one number to loosen if so.
5Changed

The trial shootout is always 2-2 before your kick

Problem

Harry: "at least have 3 penalties scored". Before your kick the score could be 0-0, 1-1 or 2-2, about a third each. At 0-0 that was five misses in a row before you could take yours.

Why

The score before your kick was picked at random.

Fix

Both team-mates score. The Academy side miss one of their three. The score is always 2-2 when it is your turn.

Shootouts at 2-2 before your kick33% 100%
How we know: seen: 2-2 before the kick on 5 of 5 shootouts on screen. The old "a third each" is the earlier design, not a new count.
6Changed

The tour has a Later button

Problem

Some tour steps make you press one button, and they blocked every other tap. You could not leave the Home screen until you pressed Play.

Why

A step that says "tap Play" dims the screen and only lets that one tap through.

Fix

A Later button on the tour card hides the tour. It comes back the next time that screen opens. This is a first step on your ask to fix the tour flow. It is not the full fix.

How we know: seen: tapped Later on screen, then the next picture.
7Changed

Commentary goes up to 8× speed

Problem

The speed button stopped at 4×.

Why

The button only had three speeds.

Fix

The speed button now goes 1× → 2× → 4× → 8×. At 8× there are about 3 seconds between your chances.

How we know: measured on screen: 3 gaps between chances, 2.9 to 3.0 seconds each.

Fixed

3Fixed

Last season's play-off result carried into every later season

Problem

Last year's play-off winner was promoted again, from wherever they finished: 15th in League Two and 19th in the Championship were both seen in a 14-season simulation. No new play-off was ever held.

Why

The play-off result was never cleared at the end of a season, so the game thought this year's play-off was already done.

Fix

The result is now cleared every season. A new play-off is held each year.

A new test fails without the fix and passes with it.

How we know: measured: a 14-season simulation before the fix, and the new test.
8Fixed

Boots wore out with no warning

Problem

Your boots wore out and nothing warned you.

Why

Nothing told you the boots were on their last match.

Fix

  • Before kick-off: the line-up screen says "Last match in these boots" when this is their last match.
  • After the match they die: the full-time screen says "Your boots just wore out. Buy a new pair in the Shop before your next match."
  • New UI only. The Old UI has no warning.
How we know: seen: both messages on screen. A first try put the pre-match line on an old screen nobody sees any more. The browser check caught it, and it now sits on the real line-up.

Known issues

New or still open from this round

  • The security database fix is still waiting. Two SQL files (security_rls_hardening_jul2026.sql, security_user_profiles_columns_aug2026.sql) are not run in Supabase. Locking the menus hides the buttons. It does not stop someone who talks to the database directly. blocked on running them
  • Player money and stats are still trusted from the saved career. A player can edit their own save. This needs a server fix before real money is involved.
  • Tester access is not built yet. Until it is, only admin accounts see the cheat menu and the test pages on the live site.

  • From v0.26: the 3D kick foot for left-footers, the signing hands, the long-hair model, the shop walk being a slowed jog.
  • Still to run in Supabase (highest first, after the two security files above): fix_two_digit_fifa_years.sql, draft_records_full_fix.sql, perf_indexes_jul2026.sql, sofifa_search_indexes.sql, fc27_clone_lower_leagues.sql.
