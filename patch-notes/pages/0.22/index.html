Pitch look

A second look for the whole game, switched in Settings. Before = Classic (today), After = Pitch, same save, same taps.

What makes New Star Soccer feel like football. The menus sit on grass: mown stripes in two greens instead of a dark gradient, so the eye reads “football” before a word. Panels are flat and outlined with thin chalk-white lines, not glowing glass. Your club’s colours do the highlighting, not a house purple. Names, numbers and headings are in tall heavy capitals, like a matchday programme. Reasoned from store pages and reviews, not measured; the store screenshots could not be viewed here.

The seven points
  • Purple/navy page gradients become striped pitch green with a floodlight vignette.
  • Glass cards become deep-green panels with a chalk-white edge.
  • Accents stay club-kit colours (already how the game themes itself).
  • Headings, big numbers and nav labels use Anton, a condensed face the site already loads.
  • Layout, animations and sizes are untouched.
