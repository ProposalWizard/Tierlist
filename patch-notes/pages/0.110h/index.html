WHAT SUPABASE AND CLOUDFLARE ARE FOR US

Short version of the two explainer pages. Supabase is the brain. Cloudflare is the warehouse and the front door.

A drawing of the idea, not a built system.

Supabase: the brain

1Live updates free

Like a group chat for screens: one screen sends, every open screen shows it at once. It already runs the Team room and draft rooms.

For us: online play that goes in turns (draft picks, one chance each, live leaderboards). Not fast action at 60 frames a second.
Left: a message every few seconds. Right: a message every frame.
2Timed jobs free

An alarm clock inside the database. It runs a job every night, hour or week.

For us: the nightly draft-room tidy, the morning health post, a nightly check for broken careers, tidying old guest accounts.
3Small servers free · 500,000 runs a month

A tiny helper next to the database that wakes when called. Like a night porter: takes a message, passes it on.

For us: passing a Team room message to a bot within a second; posting new bug reports into #bugs.
A message in, a bot out.
4Row rules free · in use

A bouncer on every table. Before showing or changing a row, it checks who you are.

For us: the site's browser key is public. The rules are the only thing that stops someone reading or changing another player's career.
Your own row: in. Someone else's row: stopped.
5The free plan staying on free

One small shared computer runs the database, with a 500 MB limit. Every big table competes for it, which is why the FIFA shrink matters.

Honest gap: downloadable daily backups are paid on Supabase. The free fix is a nightly copy to Cloudflare (your call below).

Cloudflare: the warehouse and the front door

1R2 file storage in use today

A big cupboard on the internet for files. It never charges when someone opens one. Marketing videos and patch-note pictures already live there.

Bar length = share of each free limit. Supabase counts downloads (5 GB a month free); R2 does not.
2Turnstile, the "are you human" check worth adding

A free check that tells people from bots, usually with no puzzle.

For us: guest accounts make bots easy. Turnstile sits in front of the guest sign-up and the bug form.
3Picture resizing not used yet

Ask for a picture at the size you need. Cloudflare shrinks it once and keeps the small copy close to the player.

For us: player faces on a phone. A face shown 60 px wide does not need a 400 px file. Free for 5,000 different sizes a month.
4Workers and Pages maybe

Workers are tiny programs that run close to the player. Pages host plain web pages that load almost at once.

For us: guarding private files, and fast landing pages for ads. Not for game logic: Vercel and Supabase already sit together in Frankfurt.
