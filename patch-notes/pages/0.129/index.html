Four parts of Leo's update, each with its own switch (Settings → Updates → Leo's update, off by default). The switch now follows your account, so the fast feed is instant on PC too. Fixed

Instant feed on PC

Problem: on PC the feed and goal videos took seconds. Why: the update switch was saved on each device, so it was still off on the PC. Fix: the switch now saves with your career and follows your account. Measured on desktop Chrome: feed opens in 0.05 s, video starts 0.04-0.07 s after the tap (was about 5 s).

Added

New commentary voice

All 92 lines re-recorded with an original broadcast-style voice (no real person copied). Goal calls now rise much more (about +10 to +17 semitones, was +4.5) and big moments are held longer; the co-commentator stays calm. Old voice is untouched when the switch is off.

Social Studio (/admin/social)

Feed, Compose, Accounts, Ideas, Video and Settings tabs: pin, hide and edit posts, write your own, make graphics, tune each account's style and how often it posts, pick each goal video's style. With nothing set, the feed is exactly as before.

Smooth goal videos

Real goals no longer move one man, stop, then the next. Frozen stretches go from 0.53 s to 0, the sharpest jerk drops from about 50 to 12, and players standing still drop from 53-81% to 9-27%.

Known issues
