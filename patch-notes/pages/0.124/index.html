Gameplay

What happens in a match. 7 changes.

  • ChangedPower 40 is a starting player now
    A full-power shot at Power 40 leaves the boot at 66 km/h (was 79). Power 65 now hits like the old 40. The top is unchanged. measured
    Power 40 shot79 → 66km/h
    1-on-1 goals at 4050 → 44%
    Long shots at 4027 → 19%
    The numbers
    • Full-power speed off the boot, metres a second, at Power 0 / 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100: now 18 / 19.5 / 21 / 23.4 / 30.6 / 32.76, before 18 / 21.6 / 25.2 / 28.8 / 32.4 / 32.76. Straight lines in between.
    • Every kick uses it: shots, passes and crosses. The fully 3D games keep their own numbers.
    • Goals measured with the same shooting thumb, 300 kicks each. At Power 65 to 80 scoring goes up a little, because fewer shots fly over the bar.
    • Pinned by tests/star/strikePower.mts.
  • ChangedChances follow your play style
    Attacking no longer gets byline crosses. Share of chances that are byline crosses: measured
    Attacking0%
    Balanced6.1%
    Defensive9.1%
    Why
    • Open-play chances came from one even deck that ignored the match: any kind, any style. A thin kind was swapped for its nearest drawing, which could be a header, so a new career saw a header in game 2.
    • Now the deal carries your play style and what is open. Measured over 120 matches for each style.
  • NewEvery byline cross has a short option
    A team-mate stands 6 to 10 m away, behind the ball and in space, for a cutback or a square ball. So weak power still has a play. measured
    The numbers
    • 300 of 300 byline crosses have him, 6 to 10 m away, behind the ball (never offside), at least 2.5 m from everybody, on screen.
    • A soft pass at Power and Technique 40 reaches him 300 times in 300.
    • He plays it himself. He used to try a lay-off back across the box, and defenders took 24 of 44.
  • ChangedNo more build-ups that end "Offside!"
    A build-up that ended in an offside flag before you touched the ball now tries another style. Only if every style ends offside does the chance start without one (about 1 in 300). measured
    Kept as a feature
    • Your own offside passes are unchanged.
    • One switch brings the build-up offside calls back (BUILDUP_OFFSIDE_CALLS in buildUp.ts).
  • NewA chance in every half on Medium and High
    If you have had no chance by 43' or 88', the ball comes to you in the final third. Not on Low, not in extra time. Halves with no chance for you: measured
    Medium20 → 0 of 600
    High19 → 0 of 600
    The numbers
    • 300 seeded matches of a weak side away. Chances a match: 3.4 → 3.5.
  • NewA new career learns its moves one at a time
    Each move opens with a guide on Home and a first-use line in the match. No header or volley in games 1 to 3 (0 in 1,285 chances). measured
    When each opens
    • Game 1: shoot (curl and chip are part of the tap), pass, play style.
    • After game 3: headers and volleys. Game 4 brings one of each for certain (by 60' and 80'). The Aerial drill opens with them.
    • Pace 60: steer your run (auto run before). Pace 65: sprint and the stamina bar.
    • After game 4: moving with the ball.
    • Built with guides but switched off for now: power shot (Power 60), knuckleball (Power 70 + Technique 60), skill moves (Technique 65), one-twos (Vision 60).
    • Saves from before this keep everything.
  • ChangedSet pieces are earned on a new career
    Penalties at 10 goals, free kicks at Technique and Vision 60, corners at 70. A team-mate takes them until then. not yet seen

Camera and sizes

How the pitch is framed. 3 changes.

  • ChangedMen, keeper and ball are one size next to the goal on every camera
    A man is 1.63 m tall everywhere: 22.2% of the goal's width (was 9% to 27%, by camera). The size of your 4-0 build-up screenshot. Cameras themselves are unchanged. seen
  • ChangedRoom to pull back: the ball a full drag from both sides
    The camera slides, or widens a little, so you can always drag to full power. It never loses the goal or anyone it showed. measured
    Tight angles21 → 0%
    Long shots18 → 0%
    Cutbacks10 → 0%
    The numbers
    • Share of chances with the ball within a full drag of a side. Now 0 of 1,600 upright chances, both framings, flat and tilted.
  • ChangedEvery cutback is side-on
    Whatever the Wide chances setting says. measured

A new career's first hour

From the trial to game 5. 10 changes.

  • NewThe first hour is a story: a person brings in each feature after a game
    A new career opens with Home, League and Play only. Old saves keep everything. seen
    1. After game 1: training day, two drills (Power and Technique), at normal energy.
    2. After game 2: the physio gives a can; a team-mate gives you his cracked phone (Messages, Fixtures, Squad, Relationships, Social). The agent texts first.

Known issues

  • The 26 Oct gameplay batch is not seen on a phone yet Low
    Power, play style, short option, room to pull back, cutbacks, offside build-ups, match labels and social. Measured in tests; the Power curve box was seen at phone size.
  • Weaker Power 40 scores fewer goals Low
    One-on-ones 50% → 44% and long shots 27% → 19% at Power 40. Judge it in play with the Power curve box.
  • Side-on cutbacks draw the men up to 5% smaller Low
    The camera widens a little to keep room to pull back, and men are a fixed size in metres.
  • Aerial training auto-played and got stuck High
    From the 26 Oct playtest. Not fixed in this version.
  • Other points from the 26 Oct playtest Low
    Header timing too quick, tutorial slow to load, no Settings guide, boots should show their stats, free-kick line green, first-goal pointer pressed a button instead of a swipe, the can purchase guide, too much too soon. The start of a career and unlock pacing are being built in a separate chat.

    None got worse in this version (checked line by line).
