Headline · Harry's camera bug

The camera framed a huge area where nobody was

Problem

At the start of some chances the picture showed mostly empty grass, with the players squeezed into the top quarter.

Why

When a chance does not use the spare striker, he is parked 400 m off the pitch. The camera still tried to fit him, gave up, and used the widest picture. This hit 5–14% of normal chances and every Kane drawing.

Fix

The camera ignores anyone parked off the pitch, and zooms to fit what the phone really shows after the tilt. Fixed in the real match, not a separate area. Nobody is cut off (0 of 277 zoomed chances).

Problems fixed

The league account called every division the Premier League
Problem

“The League @PremierLeague” posted National League South highlights.

Why

The game had one league account, and a league match with no competition name defaulted to Premier League.

Fix

The account is named after your division (e.g. National League South @NationalLeagueSouth). Wrong names: 153 of 211 → 0.

Goal videos stopped 1.5 seconds after the goal
Problem

Clips cut off just after the ball went in.

Why

The match moves on before the 2.2 s after-goal time is up, and the clip ended where recording stopped.

Fix

Every clip runs the full 2.2 s; the players ease to a stop after the last recorded frame.

A League One club could win the Champions League
Problem

Outside the Premier League, England's European places went to clubs from your own division.

Why

The European field was built from your division's table.

Fix

England's places come from the real Premier League clubs (played out in the background when you are below it). Lower-division European winners in 600 test seasons: 134 → 0.

Posts with holes in them
Problem

Lines like “-. Thank you for the support today!”, an “undefined-undefined” headline, and “Reading FC FC”.

Fix

A post is never written if its words need a fact the event doesn't have. A scan of 5,786 test posts now finds none.

Renewals passed the ★100k wage cap
Problem

A star kept renewing and reached ★271k a week by season 20.

Fix

Renewals stop at the cap. Career income for a star: ★108.8m → ★83.1m. More money findings below.

Added

Find a new club
Where

Stats → Contract → Find a new club.

What

Pick a division, ask every club or one. Each answers yes (role, wage, signing fee, years) or no with a reason (“You're not good enough for us yet”, “We have four strikers better than you”). Agree, or Talk terms. Asking costs manager goodwill (−4 for one club, −8 for all).

Moves

In the summer window, or straight away in January with “Join [club] now”: you join mid-season at the same week; cup-tied in cups you have played.

International career
Breaks

Four a season: September, October, November, March. Before the next club match: CALLED UP or NOT SELECTED, with a bar showing how close you are.

Matches

Qualifiers and friendlies, played in the real match with a national squad. Caps and goals credited once; international goals stay out of club totals. New 🌍 Country view on Stats.

Tournaments

Euros (24 nations) and World Cup (32) every two summers: squad selection, 3 group games, then knockouts with extra time and penalties. Winning puts the trophy in your cabinet. Opposing nations now have named players with faces.

The world ages
What

Every other club's players get a year older each season. Young players improve, players decline from about 30 (pace first), and retire between 33 and 40. A young newcomer with a made-up name and face replaces each one.

Measured

Over 20 test seasons each division's average stays within 2–3 points; by season 21 only 2% of the original players are left.

News

Team-mates and famous names who retire get farewell posts; promising youngsters get “one to watch” posts.

A real Ballon d'Or night
What

A stage, a host, the final three walk onto podiums, “And the winner is…”, the golden ball, confetti and a living audience. If you made the top three you see your place on stage; if not, a shorter version. Skip is always there.

Switch

Settings → Look → Ballon d'Or night: New | Old. Phones that can't run 3D get a drawn version.

Seen on the test page in a test browser (no graphics chip, so not seen moving). The numbers bottom-left are a dev-only meter.

Known issues and decisions

Money: three decisions for Harry and Mikey
Renewals

You can keep renewing above your club's own pay (★33k a week at Brentford, whose star is on ★15.6k). Proposed: stop renewals at the club's star wage.

Sponsors

Deals don't follow your wage: a star's sponsors pay more than his wage. Proposed: renewal fee = today's fair fee × the mood raise.

KIB cans

A can costs 2 weeks' wage at every level, and Basic costs twice Premium. Cans are 35–39% of a good player's spending. Proposed: 0.5 / 1 / 2 weeks.
