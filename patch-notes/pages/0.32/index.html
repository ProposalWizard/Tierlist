Headline

What you see in a match now matches what happens

Problem

Saves, touches, shots, headers and blocks all looked the same, or like nothing.

  • A team-mate's shot made your figure kick.
  • A defender cleared the ball with no leg moving.
  • Every keeper save was the same dive.
Why

The match had four outfield poses: idle, run, kick, receive. One shared kick timer was read only by your own figure. The engine never wrote down who touched the ball or how.

Fix
  • The engine now writes down who touched the ball and how. It only records. It changes no physics.
  • Each player has his own animation clock.
  • There is a new pose for each situation: one-hand save, catch, parry, fumble, touch, five kinds of strike, header, block, clearance.

What this means for you: it is a look-only layer, and you can switch it off. Settings → Look → Animations: New | Old (default New). Old plays the game exactly as before.

Added

Keeper saves look like different saves
Problem

Every save was the same two-arm flat dive. A catch, a parry and a fumble looked alike.

Why

The keeper had one dive pose. The engine did not say which kind of save it was. A contact flash existed in the code but was never drawn.

Fix
  • Top corner (de Gea): one glove over his head, the other arm tucked, body on a diagonal. Before: both arms flung, body flat.
  • Catch: the ball is held to his chest.
  • Parry or push: he palms it away and gets up after about 0.75 s.
  • Fumble: he gathers it, his hands fly apart, the ball spills and he starts to rise.
  • A white flash shows for about 0.2 s where the ball meets his hands.
  • On the 3D sprite, the dive clip now starts at the save moment.
A team-mate takes a touch before he plays on
Problem

A team-mate receiving the ball stood in an arms-open pose. Only the first runner did it. First-time and scrambled receptions showed nothing.

Why

One "receive" pose, tied to one runner. The engine did not record who got the ball.

Fix

He cushions the ball with his foot, thigh or chest, chosen by the ball's height. Then he sets himself. All of it fits inside the same 0.45 s as before.

A team-mate shoots or passes with his own leg
Problem

When a team-mate shot, your figure kicked. That was a bug.

Why

The kick timer was shared. Only your figure read it.

Fix
  • Each player swings his own leg, on his own clock.
  • Five shapes: driven, curl, volley, chip, side-foot pass.
  • All five get a bent standing leg, a balancing arm, a contact flash (0.16 s) and a puff of dust on the ground.
  • There is no backswing. The animation starts at contact. A wind-up would play after the ball has left.

Why

The figure is about 40 px tall in a match. Small angles do not show.

Headers, blocks and clearances move more
Problem

A header was a stand-still. A block and a clearance had no leg or body across the ball.

Fix
  • Header: a real jump (squat, up, down) over 0.55 s. Jump height 0.62 → 0.74 of the figure's radius, with a bigger arm throw.
  • Block: leg and body across the ball's path for 0.6 s. Reach 1.25×.
  • Clearance or tackle: a big hoof, swing 1.6×. A headed clearance jumps.
  • Touch: 1.25×.
3D sprite players use the existing clips better
Problem

In the 3D view only you played the kick clip.

Fix

A team-mate or defender who shoots, passes or clears plays the existing kick clip. A header lifts the sprite in code. A block tilts it. The keeper's dive clip is timed to the save. No new clips were baked.

Not seen: this has only been type-checked. See Seen / not seen.

Fixed

A caught ball stayed where the keeper stood while he slid away needs your OK
Problem

When the keeper caught a shot, the ball stayed where he caught it. He then slid away after the whistle.

Why

The keeper's move-to target was not set to the ball on a catch.

Fix

On a catch the engine now sets the keeper's target to his own position, so ball and keeper end together. Over 234 catches the gap fell from a median of 0.87 m (168 over half a metre) to a median of 0.03 m (1 over half a metre). Outcome counts are identical.
