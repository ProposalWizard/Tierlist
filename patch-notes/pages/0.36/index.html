Headline

Goal videos have sound

Problem

The goal videos in the feed (v0.10) were silent.

Why

v0.10 made only the pictures. Leo asked: "crowd noise and commentator but a mute toggle too."

Fix
  • The crowd: a murmur under the whole video. It rises as the shot comes, and roars when the ball goes in.
  • The ball: you hear each kick, the keeper's gloves and the net, on the frame the video shows them.
  • The commentator: one line just after the goal ("What a finish!"), and one over the slow-motion replay ("Let's see that again."). The same goal always gets the same line. In a reel, two goals never get the same line in a row.
  • A fan's phone video has no commentator, only the crowd round him, louder.
  • In the slow-motion replay the crowd drops back, and the kick and the net sound slower and deeper.
  • Save video keeps the sound inside the file.

Look forTurn your sound on. The kick, the saves, the net, the roar, then the commentator. The TV video ends with a replay line over the slow motion.

What this means for you: watch your goal in the feed with sound on. Tap the speaker to mute.

Check these

Added

A speaker button on every goal video
Asked for

"a mute toggle too."

Fix
  • Bottom left of the video. Tap to mute, tap again for sound. It mutes every goal video, not one.
  • It is remembered on this phone. The first time, it follows Settings → Sound effects: if you switched the game's sounds off, the videos start muted.
  • Some phones do not allow sound before you tap the screen. Then the video plays muted and the speaker shows muted; one tap on it turns the sound on.
Nine commentator lines, and the Sound Board can replace them
Problem

The game had no commentator voice at all.

Fix
  • Nine short lines, made with a free British voice that runs offline. 3 to 11 KB each.
  • They are on the Sound Board (/admin/sound-board) in a new group, "Goal video commentator". An admin can replace any line with a better recording, and the videos use it straight away.
  • The kick, net, gloves and stadium roar on the Sound Board now say they play in goal videos.

Known issues

The commentator sounds calm, not excited
Why

The free voice reads; it does not shout. A real "GOOOAL!" needs a recorded voice.

Status

Replace the lines on the Sound Board with better takes (an ElevenLabs voice, or one of you). No code change needed.

The commentator does not say names
Why

Each line is a recording made in advance. A recording cannot know the scorer.

Status

Open. Names would need a recorded name for each player, or a voice made on the phone (which cannot go into a saved file).

Not heard on an iPhone
Why

Checked in a test browser only (it made a WebM with sound; the files on this page were turned into MP4).

Status

Worked out from the code, not seen: an iPhone that cannot put sound in the file still plays the same sound next to the video, but a saved video from that phone is silent.
