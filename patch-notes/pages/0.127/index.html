2 · CAREER AND SEASON (MIKEY)

  • The world season: every league and cup is played for real
    Problem: only your own league was played. Fix: 7 English leagues, both cups, both shields, the Champions League and the Europa League play match by match on their real dates. Promotion and relegation come from those tables. League Two beat a Premier League club in a cup 34% of the time; now 8%. v0.114.
  • FA Cup: ten real rounds, each league joins at its real round
    Was 6 rounds. 24 byes in the 3rd Qualifying Round, never your club. 20,029 soak matches, 0 broken states. v0.116.
  • Transfer window: clubs come to you each summer
    Your season gets a letter, S to D. The letter decides how many leagues up a club can come from. League → Club → Negotiate. Fee priced by your league (a non-league striker no longer shows ★406m). v0.59.
  • The world ages
    Players decline from 31, retire at 35 to 41 (keepers 37 to 43), and young players from the same country replace them. 211 nations wear their real kits (was 15). v0.59.
  • New pop-ups: Settings → Match → Pop-up pack: Classic | Broadcast
    Broadcast is a goal scoreboard with a rolling score, scorer and assist, and TV captions. Default Classic. v0.116, v0.100h.
  • Game music: 11 original orchestral tracks
    Off by default. Home, matches, full time and the Ballon d'Or night. 17% level in a match, 32% at home. v0.117.
  • Mikey's master plan, steps 1 to 5
    One bottom bar everywhere and a cleaner Home; Stats shows the whole card and four tabs; League is one page for every competition; the Phone holds Relationships, Squad, Fixtures & results and real messages; the phone clock shows the real day. Phone back to Home 1.9 s → 0.3 s. v0.120.

3 · THE MATCH (HARRY)

  • Running after a chance (B14)
    Problem: a pass ended your part of the chance. Fix: it plays on in the same picture. Auto run on a phone, W A S D and Shift on a computer, and a stamina bar. 500 matches: your goals 0.98 → 1.04 a match. v0.95h, v0.96h.
  • Headers and volleys are back, then made real
    Back on after being off since 24 Sep (v0.88). Then 52 real headers and 102 real volleys play in the air, not along the ground (v0.105h). Cross heights now vary 1.5 to 2.6 m (was always 1.85 m) (v0.106h).
  • New skill: Aerial & Acrobatics, with a Head & Volley drill
    30 drill levels. In real matches, headers and volleys read Aerial, not Technique. Goals per 100 headers at Aerial 30 / 60 / 90: 14.1 / 24.9 / 36.3. A gold perfect circle that grows with Aerial. v0.105h, v0.106h, v0.108h.
  • Build-ups before chances
    Built behind a switch (v0.63h, v0.86, v0.93), cut from 32.2 to 21.6 s a match (v0.103h), then about 3 s each in three styles: long ball, tiki-taka, or a run then a pass. Real moments about 1 in 10 chances (was 1 in 7). On by default for new phones. v0.107h, v0.108h.
  • Player size: up to C, then back to B
    Size C made men 1.56x bigger (v0.103h). It was too big, so they went back to B: about 22 px on a phone long-range chance (C was 28). Headers, volleys and the drill drew men 1.2 to 1.45x too big; now 22 px too. v0.107h, v0.108h.
  • Fairer even matches (sim piece 4)
    One shared builder for match inputs. Even-match possession 44.5% → 49.8%. v0.106h.
  • Shots miss a third less; a midweek game pays a quarter-week extra
    Mikey's playtest rounds, 22 Oct. Also: a player taken off in extra time stays off. v0.120.
