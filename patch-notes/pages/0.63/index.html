Phase 2: a new build-up before every open-play chancemeasuredseen
Problem / Why

The notes give no problem. You asked (answer 13) that Infinite Highlights and Infinite Match play build-ups, “automatic and natural, with some adjusting”.

Fix

With the switch On, each open-play chance starts with a 4 to 8 second build-up. It is different every time and ends exactly on the drawn chance. The moves come from real attacking patterns in the free match data. Better passers play the long balls. Better dribblers make the runs.
