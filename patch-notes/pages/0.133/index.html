Take a touch

On a live chance you can now take ONE touch: a quick flick from the ball, the way you want it to go. You run onto it and finish. In a one-on-one the keeper now rushes you: take it round him and he goes down; push it at him and he smothers it. Inside Live chances, which is still off by default.

Made by Leo · 1 Nov 2026

WHAT YOU ASKED FOR

“You get played through one on one with the goalie and he’s running towards you and just as the ball ends up at your feet you do a little swipe like a bit forwards and to the left on the ball to take it round the keeper and slot it into the open goal.” One touch only. Your picks: a quick flick, the keeper rushes, it stays live, every live chance.

HOW IT PLAYS

1. Flick = touch, drag back = aimseen

A quick flick from the ring (under a quarter of a second) is a touch in the flick’s direction. A longer flick is a heavier touch. A slower drag back is still your aim, as always. One touch per chance; a second flick does nothing. A small tip shows on your first 5 live chances: “Drag back = aim · Quick flick = take a touch”.

2. Timed like a strikemeasured

Flick as the ball reaches the ring: a clean touch goes where you flicked. Early or late, it comes off heavier and up to 16° off line (“HEAVY TOUCH” when it’s bad). Out of reach: you miss it.

3. Round the keeperseen

In a one-on-one (and through balls) the keeper now comes off his line at you as the pass arrives. Take it round him: he dives where the ball was going, stays down a beat, then scrambles back. You run onto it, the ring moves with you, and you slot it into the open goal (aim, let go, kick screen as usual).

4. Push it at him and he smothers itseen

If he can get to the ball before you, he goes down on it: SAVED, the chance is gone.

The numbers

Measured over 23 real one-on-ones, keeper rushing: a medium touch round him is smothered 22% of the time, a soft one 26%, a heavy one 9%, a square one 0%. Straight at him: 91%. Into his side: 70%. He rushes at 4.4–6.0 m/s by rating and reacts to your touch in 0.20–0.32 s.

5. Any live chance: open up a better angleseen

On any live chance, a touch moves you to a better spot. Here a touch to the right on a long-range chance, then a shot in. The camera glides with you (never cuts).

6. Don’t dawdleseen

Once you’ve caught it up it’s at your feet and you carry it. Shoot within about 2.5 s or you’re CLOSED DOWN.
