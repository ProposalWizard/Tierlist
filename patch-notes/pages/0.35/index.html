Headline

Goals in the feed are real videos now

Problem

After a match, some posts looked like videos: a picture with a play button. Nothing played. A goal replay played the chance again with new luck, so it could end differently from your real goal.

Why

The game kept who scored and when, but not how the goal happened. A replay could only set the chance up again and play it again.

Fix
  • The match records every goal while you play: the ball, every player and the keeper's dive, 30 times a second.
  • After the match, a post about a goal plays that recording as a real video. Tap ▶. The phone makes the video in a few seconds (a ring shows how far), then plays it on a loop, like a real feed.
  • Save video: on a phone, the share menu opens (Save Video puts it in Photos, or send it in a message). On a computer, it downloads.
  • Every play and every save is the same goal, frame for frame. Nothing is played again with dice.

Every goal is recorded as you play
Asked for

"The goal replays should always be the same, not just remaking the situation."

Fix
  • What: the ball, every player on the screen, the keeper's dives and saves, and who touched the ball when. 30 times a second, from the first kick of the move to just after the goal (12 seconds at most).
  • Size: one goal is 9 to 15 KB.
  • Where: on this phone only, in the browser's own storage. Not in the career save, so the save that goes to the cloud stays the same size.
  • How many: the newest 80 goals, plus every goal kept in Goal Replays.

Measured: in the video, every player and the ball are within 5 cm of where they really were (test). Goals by you and by team-mates are both recorded.

Asked for

"Maybe even different angles to make them look like real social media posts."

Fix
  • TV (your club): high in the stand at the side, following the ball. A score bar at the top, a GOAL banner, the scorer and the minute.
  • Behind the goal (highlights pages): low behind the net. You see the keeper's back and the net bulge.
  • A fan in the stand (fans and meme pages): a tall phone video, a bit shaky, heads in front, the account's @name in the corner.
  • The crowd wears the two clubs' colours. Some matches are at night.

Fixed (found while checking)

The keeper stood on his head in the fan's video
Problem

From the fan's camera, a keeper's dive could look upside down.

Why

The players' drawings are made for the match's own camera, which looks down from above. A dive that runs down the screen reads as a man on his head.

Fix

A dive is drawn at most 35° from flat, towards the ball. Tested over 2,701 ways he can face and dive: none steeper than 35°, every one to the right side.

  • A post about one goal could play the match's other goals
    When that goal was not recorded. Now it plays that goal, or shows a picture. Over 30 simulated matches, no post plays a wrong goal (test).

Known issues

  • Recordings stay on the phone that played the match
    Another phone shows the post's picture. On purpose: it keeps the cloud save small.
  • Goals from before this update have no recording
    Their posts are pictures, and their replays play the old way.
  • The first play takes a few seconds
    The phone makes the video then: 1.3 to 3.1 seconds on this slow test machine. After that it plays at once, until you leave the game.
  • No sound yet
    The videos are silent.
  • Only goals in the real match are recorded
    Simulated matches, five-a-side and training drills record nothing.
  • Not seen on an iPhone
    An iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or later to make the video (worked out, not seen). Older phones show the picture.
  • Goal Replays is for admins only
    As before. Players see their goals in the feed.
  • The celebration in a video is short
    A recording stops when the match moves on, about 1.3 to 1.8 seconds after the goal (seen in the real game).
  • "The League" posts lower-league matches as Premier League older
