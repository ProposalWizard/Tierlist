N1Fixedlive on the site

Strike screen "Where do you strike it?": the whole ball takes a tap now

Problem

  • Only the bottom of the ball worked. On Harry's iPhone, only the bottom 25% of the ball took a tap.
  • A see-through halo sat round the ball.
  • Not on a computer. It does not happen there, so it was hard to find.

Why reasoned, not seen

The match picture behind the strike screen is tipped back 20° in 3D. iPhone Safari can let that tipped picture catch taps that should reach the ball. The halo is most likely the ball's shadow effect. We could not reproduce either problem on a computer.

Fix

  • The whole screen listens for the tap and checks where it landed against the ball.
  • A tap just outside the ball, up to the countdown ring, hits the nearest edge of the ball.
  • The tipped pitch is hidden while the strike screen is up, so it cannot catch taps.
  • The shadow is a plain round one. That should remove the halo.

The headline: 3D people are one body, with fingers

The signing scene, the shop and the garden used three different bodies. Now they use one, and it has fingers.

Problem

  • Three bodies. The signing scene, the 3D shop and the garden each showed your player with a different body.
  • A bodybuilder shape. The waist was 0.27 m under shoulders 0.46 m wide.
  • No fingers. The pen floated beside the hand. In the handshake the two hands stopped 7 cm apart.

Why

Each scene loaded its own body. The hand was one flat piece with no finger joints, so nothing could close round a pen or grip another hand.

Fix

  • One body everywhere: the signing scene, the shop, the garden, and the 3 team-mates on the garden bench.
  • A normal shape: the waist is 0.30 m and the shoulders are about 2 cm narrower.
  • 15 finger joints on each hand. The fingers now bend.
  • The pen: the fingers close round it.
  • The handshake: the hands now meet and grip.
  • The switch: Settings → Look → "3D people: New | Old". The old bodies are still there under Old.

6Changed

The match engine can change now, with a warning

Problem

Mikey's rule said never change the match engine. So two match bugs could not be fixed: the wall chases the loose ball after a deflected free kick, and a cutback along the byline sits for 1.6 s before it is called intercepted.

Why

The rule had no way to say yes. Any engine change was against it, even a small one that fixed a real bug.

Fix

  • The ban is gone. This was Harry's call.
  • A warning instead. A check warns every time any session changes the engine.
  • The rules for a change. Name it in the patch notes with before and after numbers, and run the match tests.
  • The two bugs will now be fixed in the engine itself (see In progress).
How we know: decision by Harry. Not seen: the warning firing. This page records the new rule; no engine change is in v0.30.
