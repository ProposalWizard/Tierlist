4 · MATCH, FEED AND TEST PAGES (LEO)

  • Free kicks: the very top corner beats the keeper
    A perfect ball into the very top corner went in 11% of the time against a 92-rated keeper; now 79%. An ordinary taker 10% → 16%. Low and middle shots unchanged. In every match. PR #828, v0.135.
  • Test pages play like the real game
    Six test screens had the old, easier free-kick keeper; test screens used skill 40 for free kicks. Both now match the real match. Weather, build-ups and sim90 too. PR #829, v0.136.
  • Live chances: take a touch, everyone moves, your stats decide
    One quick flick to open an angle; the keeper rushes one-on-ones; defenders close you down and can win it. Technique, pace, vision and power set the skill. Still off by default. PRs #820, #823, #826, #827; v0.126, v0.133, v0.134.
  • Social feed: football Twitter and TikTok
    52 made-up accounts and 899 lines (was 33 and 272); replies that fit the post (mismatches 20.7% → 0.1%); polls, quote posts, a ranked feed. A Social Studio admin page at /admin/social. Behind Leo's switch. PRs #821, #822, #824, #825; v0.129 to v0.132.
  • Goal videos and commentary
    Goal videos start in 0.05 s on PC (was about 5 s); full screen; 2D from above; cut five ways plus six new edit types. A new commentary voice and four commentary teams. The season video plays every goal (0 lost clips, was 8 of 21). Behind Leo's switch. v0.129 to v0.132.
