Added: 3D training drills

Addedlive on the siteonly stills seen

The training gate now opens a picker with five 3D drills

Problem

The training pitch had one drill: the Crossbar challenge. There was nothing to practise passing, heading, volleys or tackling.

Why

Each 3D game would have needed its own movement and ball rules. They would have drifted apart from each other and from the match.

Fix

  • Training gate → Training Pitch opens a drill picker. A big "Random drill" button, then Crossbar Challenge (unchanged), Two Touch, Free Roam, Headers & Volleys and Wembley (Normal or Doubles).
  • One shared 3D engine for all 3D games. Movement, touches, passes, juggling, headers, volleys, tackles and the keeper live in one place, so the drills cannot drift apart.
  • Shots use the same drag-and-aim maths as the 2D match. Measured difference in kick speed, lift and spin: 0.0%.
  • Rule recorded: 3D games get their own rules folder and never touch the 2D match.

The drills

Two Touch

  • Keep it up with a team-mate. Each of you gets two touches at most.
  • A third touch, or the ball hitting the grass, ends the rally.
  • Best of 3 rallies. 6 or more passes wins.
Mate rated 85: passes per rally5.4
Mate rated 55: passes per rally2.1

200 rallies for each rating.

Free Roam

  • Thumb stick to move. Tap to pass. Drag back to shoot.
  • 2 minutes on half a pitch, with a keeper and two team-mates.
  • Score = goals + clean passes. 10 or more wins.

Headers & Volleys

  • Your real left-back and right-back cross 10 balls.
  • A yellow ring shows where each ball lands.
  • Header goal 2 points. Volley goal 3. Any other goal 1. 7 points wins.
Goals per 10 vs a 60-rated keeper3.6
Goals per 10 vs an 80-rated keeper2.5

An average player.

Wembley (Harry's rules)

  1. 3 to 6 players, or 2 to 3 pairs, on half a pitch.
  2. The keeper throws the ball out. It is a free for all.
  3. Score and you are safe for the round.
  4. The last player without a goal in a round is out.
  5. Rounds repeat until one player is left.
4-player game, minutes10 4.0

Average, before and after tuning. 0 of 200 test games stalled.

  • No stuck games: after 60 seconds with no goal the keeper drops a little and the screen says so. It never hands out a goal.

New hand-made moves

  • Standing and diving header, volley, first touch, chest and thigh control.
  • Sprint, dribble run, lofted pass, poke and sliding tackle.
  • Keeper dives, high catch, throw-out and ready shuffle. A safe jog-off and an out walk-off.
  • Each is timed to the frame the foot, head or hands meet the ball.
  • Cost: the 3D shop and garden download about 150 KB more.

Also fixed

  • The hint line at the bottom of the 3D drill screen was cut off on a phone. It now fits.

Not done yet

  • Keepy-up headers and foot keepy-ups still borrow other moves.
  • The keeper has no move for catching a low ball.
  • Your own shot from your feet has no wind-up.
How we know: seen: the stills above only. Measured: the 0.0% shot difference, the Two Touch, Headers & Volleys and Wembley numbers. Not seen: any drill in motion, or on a phone.

Fixed: cheat locks, with no lag and no new database cost

Fixedlive on the siteMikey runs SQL

Four places that trusted the phone now check what they are sent

Problem

The public Draft records board, legend share links, XP rewards and the Hall of Fame all took whatever the phone sent. Someone could post a fake record, a fake career, or collect the same reward again and again.

Why

The game was built to trust the phone. v0.34 locked the save and the wallet. These four were next on its list.

Fix

  • Draft records board (public): accepts only possible numbers (38 league games at most, 63 wins at most across all competitions), a number that matches your saved season, and real player names.
  • Legend share links: copy the career from your own Hall of Fame. 30 shares at most.
  • XP: the server picks the reward key, so each thing pays once. Join once. Daily login once a day. Draft rewards need a real saved season.
  • Hall of Fame: 30 careers at most.
Honest cases that could be refused
  • A record with an odd-character name loses that one record.
  • A Draft season whose history save failed (no signal) skips its records.
  • More than 10 Ballon d'Or seasons in one day stop paying XP.

Run star_hall_of_fame.sql, star_legend_shares.sql and (optional) xp_atomic_award.sql. The order is in the red box at the top.
