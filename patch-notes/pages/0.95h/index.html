ADDED: THE RUN AFTER YOUR PASS

Your pass no longer ends your part in the moveseenmeasured
Problem

After you passed in open play, the chance ended for you. Only Chances: New with a build-up played on.

Why

The run-on code sat behind that one setting.

Fix

Any pass in open play with a goal in the picture now plays on in the same picture, whatever the Chances setting. Not on shots, corners, free kicks or penalties. The two old play-on branches in the match are replaced by this one run.

Auto run on a phone, keys on a computerseen
Problem

There was no way to move your player after a pass.

Fix

Auto run (phone, and whenever you do not steer) takes you into the best space it can see: onside, toward goal, a sprint when it is far, and a sprint onto a ball played back. On a computer W A S D (or the arrows) steer you and Shift sprints. Let go and Auto run takes over after 0.6 s. The keys only belong to the match while your run is live, so they never touch the aim. The After you pass: Joystick setting keeps its thumb stick, now on the same legs and bar.

The man on the ball can play it back to youmeasuredseen
Problem

The team-mate who took your pass always finished the move himself.

Fix

He shoots, plays it to your feet, or slides you in. It depends on how much better your spot is than his and how good a passer he is. No captaincy needed (captains still get Call for it). You reach 85% of the balls played back. If you were onside when you passed, you are offside when he looks up 2.5% of the time.

Ball back, passer with passing 88— → 15.4%

CHANGED: STAMINA IS YOUR ENERGY

One bar, tied to your match energyseenmeasured
Problem

Sprint stamina and match energy were two separate things.

Fix

The bar is the 3D games' sprint bar: 6 s of sprinting from full to empty at Physical 50, and it refills when you ease off. It can never refill past your match energy: at energy 60 it stops at 60%, and the part past that is hatched. Sprinting costs real match energy, 0.12 a second (a full 6 s sprint = 0.7), taken off the same energy the career saves. Speeds come from your pace: pace 50 runs 5.1 and sprints 6.9 m/s; pace 90 sprints 7.9 m/s.
