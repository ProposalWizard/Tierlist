Live 3D scenes

1New

The signing is a live 3D scene, and it is your player

Problem

The signing was a flat picture. You asked for a live 3D scene with real movement: pick up the pen, write, shake hands.

Fix

  • A rigged 3D body wears your skin tone, your face picture (or a fake face), your club kit with your number, and your accessories from the Store and the Star Pass.
  • He picks up the pen, writes his name, the SIGNED stamp lands, then he shakes the manager's hand. About 5 seconds. Tap to skip.
  • The contract shows your real club, number and wage.
  • Hair style and colour are now picked on the profile screen and saved. Old saves get short brown hair.
  • Polish round: the manager is fully dressed from behind, no torn cloth in the handshake, the hands meet palm to palm, the face sits on the head (not a sticker), normal arms, and a real pen grip.
  • It is off by default. Turn it on in Career Settings → 3D signing scene (beta). If it is off, you get the old drawn scene.
How we know: seen in stills at phone size, dark and light skin, in the 3D Test Area. The career uses the same code.
2New

Walk round a 3D shop

Problem

You asked for the old 3D shop in the game, as a button, with the real models bigger and a better-looking shop. The normal Store stays.

Fix

  • New button on the Shop page: Walk the 3D shop (beta).
  • Real 3D boots (7) and cars (6), bigger. Watches and jewellery hang as pictures in light boxes.
  • Showroom lighting. Tap an item to look at it. 'See it in the shop' takes you to the normal shop to buy it. Nothing is bought in 3D.
How we know: seen in stills at phone size.

The first minutes

4Changed

You play your first game before the tutorials

Problem

The start explained every page before you played. You said: play the first game first, then the boss, then training. The Shop should not open after game 1.

Fix

  • New career: League, Stats and Play are open. Training, Relations and Phone are locked.
  • The welcome tour is 3 steps: energy, Low/Medium/High, then "You've got a game today. Tap Play".
  • After game 1: Training and Achievements open. Then "Your manager wants a word. Tap Relations" opens the boss meeting.
  • The Shop opens after the boss meeting and two drills. Every tour is 3 steps or fewer.
How we know: seen in a browser; new tests check the order.

Trial and match

9Changed

Shootout: full pitch and a scorecard

Problem

The shootout sat on a white card, and both team-mates could miss the same way.

Fix

  • No white card. The pitch is full width.
  • A scoreboard above the pitch: a ✓ or ✗ for each kick, the score, who is next, and a ★ on your kick.
  • Team-mates never miss the same way twice. They shoot to opposite sides.
How we know: measured: both team-mates missed the same way in 554 of 1,660 shootouts before, 0 of 5,000 after.
10Changed

A defender who wins the ball clears it away from his own goal

Problem

You saw a defender "shoot" at his own keeper. When he won a loose ball, the game left the ball rolling, often at his own goal.

Fix

When a defender wins the ball, or a rebound hits him, the ball now goes up the pitch at the same speed. A shot blocked by a defender can still go in at an odd angle, as you asked in v0.15.

11New

Left foot or right foot

Problem

The foot you chose did nothing.

Fix

  • Your chosen foot swings the kick. A left-footed kick is the mirror of a right-footed one.
  • On a penalty or a direct free kick, a left-footer runs up from the right of the ball.
  • Team-mates and opponents get a fixed foot each; about 1 in 5 are left-footed.
  • Only the picture changes. The ball, the aim and the shot do not.
How we know: Being fixed now (you said it isn't right). A builder is checking every kick in a real browser; a short clip follows here.

16Changed

Settings on the title screen is for this phone only

Problem

17Fixed

Full screen on iPhone

Problem

Full screen did not work for you or Mikey.

Fix

  • iPhone Safari does not let a web page go full screen. The way round it is Add to Home Screen: the game then opens with no browser bars.
  • The game is now set up for that (app name and icons). On iPhone, Settings → Full screen shows "How?" with the 3 steps.
  • On Android, if full screen drops, the next tap brings it back.
  • Exit career and Delete career now use the game's own pop-up. The browser's pop-up was what knocked you out of full screen.
How we know: seen in a phone-sized browser. Not tried on a real iPhone or Android.
18Fixed

Fewer sounds, and quiet taps on iPhone

Problem

Sounds played too often, and taps sounded loud on iPhone.

Fix

  • Only green and gold buttons (Continue, Confirm, Play) make a sound: 37 of 140 buttons.
  • The same sound can't repeat straight away; no more than 3 sounds start at once.
  • iPhone Safari ignored the volume on quiet taps. Sounds now go through one output where the volume works.
  • Settings → Sound effects off now also mutes the match.
How we know: rate limit measured (5 quick pops: 5 → 1). iPhone part worked out from the code, not tried on a phone.
