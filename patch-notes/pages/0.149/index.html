The 60-day launch, the studio and the Discord

Made by Harry · 28 Nov 2026 · a plan for Mikey and Leo to see and react to · nothing in the game changes from this page

CHECK THESE

1 · THE 60 DAYS

Days 1–30 are a closed beta. You get in only through Discord, with a code. Days 31–60 are an open beta on the website. The phone app comes on day 60.

The safe version. The game is already built. On day 31 only part of it is switched on. Each morning the next set of features switches on, and Discord gets "here's what went in today, please test it". It is one copy of the game, so a bug fix still reaches everyone at once.

2 · UPDATES ONLY ADD, SO NOBODY RESTARTS

A game update can add or fix a thing. It can never take a finished feature away. So a save from the closed beta still loads in the app on day 60.

Saves already live in our database: three slots per signed-in account. Old saves already upgrade themselves (an old six-round FA Cup save keeps its own rounds). Every new feature must do the same. At launch the leaderboards reset; careers carry on.

3 · FROM A VIDEO TO A FOUNDING PLAYER

Videos bring people in. Discord keeps them. In the closed beta, Discord is the only door, so the ask on every video is "get a beta code in our Discord".

From day 31 the order flips: the first ask on a video is "play now in your browser", and Discord becomes the second ask, inside the game ("Found a bug? Want a say? Join the Discord"). Why: only a few viewers ever join a Discord. In the research, one game's 1.9-million-view video brought 120 people into its Discord.

6 · CODES, WAVES AND SAFETY

Codes live in the game, not in Discord. People come in waves, under rules everyone can read. Football banter is fine; abuse is a ban.

  • One-use codes, tied to one account
    A used code cannot be resold, and the game knows who invited whom. The Discord role only opens the channels.
  • Wave rules in public
    For example: "20 places every 3–4 days, by sign-up order". People get angry at hidden picking, not at waiting.
  • Email opt-in from day 1
    One unticked box at sign-in: "Tell me when the app and Steam launch". Email beats Discord on launch day.
  • Google Play test by about day 40
    Google needs 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row before a new app goes public. We aim for 20–25 Android testers from the Discord, to cover drop-outs.
  • UK safety habits
    Moderators 18+ only, no access to the admin or emails · written rules: banter yes, abuse a ban · no age-restricted channels (teen accounts cannot see them) · every giveaway is a free random draw · staff never DM links.

The numbers, if you want them

Plan: 30 days closed, 30 days open, app on day 60. About 180 posts at 3 a day.

Discord, from the research: Copa City (a small football game) gave out 200 beta keys through its Discord. Omno's 1.9-million-view TikTok brought 120 people into its Discord. Discord calls about 30% of members chatting each week "a healthy goal". Its guide says to start with about 8 channels and no more than 5 per group.

Email: one small comparison had 38–46% of email readers clicking against 27% for a Discord post (from a search preview, not fully checked).

App stores: Google Play: at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row (personal developer accounts). Apple TestFlight: up to 10,000 outside testers; builds expire after 90 days.
