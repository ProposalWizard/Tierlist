A chat bubble on every admin pageseen
Problem / Why

Your ask (19 Oct): a team room in the admin, with “live chat you can open from any admin page as a pop-up box”. Chat lives only in the admin room.

Fix

A round button, bottom left, on every admin and test page. It opens a small box with Chat and To-do. It steps clear of the side menu and of buttons. (The next round, B11, made it smaller and see-through to taps.)

Four views of the team room: Board, Ideas, Chat, To-doseen
Problem / Why

Your ask (19 Oct): an idea board and hand-typed tasks next to the live bars on the team board.

Fix

One switch on the team board: Board | Ideas | Chat | To-do. The same four sit in the side menu, with red and amber counts. The board and the bubble take the colours of the look you pick.

Ideas: a board with three stagesseen
Problem / Why

Not in the report.

Fix

Ideas sit in Idea → Doing → Done, shown by stage or by person. Each has three vote dots and notes. You can move an idea between stages.

Team chatseen
Problem / Why

The full chat page is the bigger version of the bubble. Same ask.

Fix

A live chat page: who is here now, pinned messages, pictures. It asks once which of Harry, Mikey or Leo you are.

To-do: your own jobs and jobs for each otherseen
Problem / Why

Your ask: hand-typed tasks next to the live bars.

Fix

A list for jobs given to you, your own jobs and jobs you gave. A ticked job fades after a day, then leaves.

Notes on pushesno still
