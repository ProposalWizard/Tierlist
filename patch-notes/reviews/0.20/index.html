Star rating out of 100Built · in test

Built in the test copy. Seen in a browser at 390 px; pace measured on 18 played-out careers with the real game functions. Old saves convert on load (2.1 → 21).

  • One rating out of 100, Overall goneseenHome, the star screen, your row in the squad and the match screen now show one Star Rating out of 100. Overall is off your screens (team-mates and opponents keep their own). Everything Overall used to drive in the background — selection, wages, transfers, call-ups and so on — still works exactly as before.
  • Why +68 moved you 1.1 stars: found and fixedmeasured + seenThe card only listed the match's own points. On your exact case it said +68 but 688 were banked: a Premier League debut bonus (500 — almost a whole old star) and two achievements. The card now lists every line and they add up.
  • A much steeper climb, to your numbersmeasuredLevels 11 → 20 now cost 108,000 points (you said about 100,000); 81 → 90 cost 9.9 million (you said about 10 million). A first match moves you 0–2 levels (was 3–6). The 90s are the ten Legend tasks, one level each.
  • Gates kept, in the new numbersmeasured29, 39, 49, 59, 69, 79, 89. Points still bank behind a gate and pay out when it opens.
  • Popup no longer says “Non-league regular”seenIt shows at every ten (20, 30…), e.g. “★ 20 · Making a name — your career so far, out of 100. It never goes down.” The band names never mention a division.
  • “What is the star rating for?”seenThe star screen now opens with one line saying what it's for: your one rating, built from everything you do, never goes down, and the Star Pass will be built on it.
Your call: World Cup gate?Not added: a World Cup only comes every 4th season and needs a call-up, so it would block players from smaller nations. Add it, or leave it?

Shop, Style and BootsBuilt · in test

Built in the test copy. Seen in a browser at 390 px with a real purchase made from the new detail sheet. Prices, fame, item ids and buying rules unchanged; old saves load.

  • Shop page: your four firstseenKIB Cans, Boots, Style and Sponsors are now big picture tiles at the top, each with a live line (cans owned, boot matches left, fame from your stuff, deals signed). Store and Casino sit underneath, half width.
  • Style page rebuiltseen + measuredGroups: Drip, Gadgets, Cars, Homes, Holiday (jet, villa, island). A three-wide grid of picture cards, fame on every card, a “Fame from your stuff” strip at the top. Longest tab 1,663 px → 686 px.
  • A real name and drawing for every levelseenAll 155 levels, no real brands — e.g. Rusty Kit Car → Used Coupé → Sports Car → Twin-Turbo GT → Track Racer; Light Aircraft → Helicopter → Small Jet → Business Jet → Private Airliner. The names are guesses — change any you like.
  • Tap an item: all 5 levels side by sideseenPictures of each level (hover previews on a computer), fame, how long it lasts, happiness, price and buy. A “Level 5 unlock — to be decided” slot, because nothing in the game unlocks there yet.
  • “My stuff”seenEverything you own in one place — boots, cans and items — with fame and seasons left. “See all” on the Shop page opens it.
  • Boots: a shop shelfseen + measuredTwo swipeable lit shelves (Everyday, Special) of drawn boots — scuffed at level 1 up to gold studs at level 5. Tap for all 5 levels, Power/Technique bars, cost per match, buy. Page 974 px → 581 px.
  • Bug fixed: fame shown wrongseenThe old Shop-page card showed an item's raw status as fame — a Level 3 phone read “+6 fame”; its real fame is +0.5. Both screens now show the real number.

  • Signing scene instead of the paper contractseen + measuredYour player (in the club kit, your face) and the manager (suit, club tie, his own face, name plate on the desk) sit at a desk with the contract and its real terms. Tap to sign → zoom in, the pen signs → handshake, confetti, “Welcome to …”. About 2.6 s, with Skip always there. “Tap to sign” is now on the first screen (before you had to scroll).

  • Training: “No more sessions this week”seenThe “1 of 2 sessions left…” sentence is gone. Sessions are two ⚡ cells, lit or spent. At zero it says “No more sessions this week”, and tapping a skill explains you get 2 more after Saturday's match (before, the cards just greyed out). The 2-a-week rule is unchanged.
  • Spin your player: a real back viewseenThe back is now the same drawn player seen from behind — surname arched over the shoulders, big number, back of the head and hair, legs. Side-on angles in between still need the 3D renders.
  • Home: the 3D/2D button now changes your player tooseenIt switched every match figure but the player standing right next to it stayed 3D. Now he flips to the flat 2D figure and back. I read your “2D flick” as this button — if you meant dragging to spin him on Home, that's a new feature (the spin page could move there).

Real-match bugsBuilt · in test

The defender who intercepts from the wrong side, and the assist you didn't make.

  • Assist you didn't make: fixedmeasuredAny team-mate goal after your pass counted as your assist, even if someone else played it in between. Now the assist goes to the last team-mate to touch it. Wrongly credited: 14% → 0; with an ordered lay-off: 75% → 0.
  • Wrong-side interception: fixed (your pick a + b)seen + measuredHe now goes round you instead of through you, and from behind he fouls 2 times in 3 (was 1 in 3); a foul in the box is a penalty. Runs through your body: 6.8% → 0 (21.4% → 0 counting the drawn figure). From behind: foul 34.5% → 71.4%, penalty 20.2% → 46.9%. From in front: unchanged. Pulls closed down overall 12.2% → 11.1%, so slightly easier. Close-down from behind takes 3.65 s → 3.90 s (median).
  • Guess to confirm: 2 in 3reasonedYou said 'more likely' with no number, so 2 in 3 is a guess. It is one setting, easy to change.
  • What you might still noticeseenWhen he passes close behind you, nearer the camera, his figure covers your legs for a moment. That is the camera angle, not him walking through you.
