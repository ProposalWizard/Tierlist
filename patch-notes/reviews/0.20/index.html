Changes, built in a test copy

Each part was built by itself, with screenshots taken before and after at phone size (390 px). Tap a picture to open it full size.

Star rating out of 100Building now

Replaces Overall on every screen (Harry, 1 Oct: “replace”). One old 0.1 step becomes one level, the scale gets much steeper, the +68 mismatch and the “Non-league regular” popup get fixed.

Trial: technique + shootoutBuilding now

Five-a-side and “find the player” come out, the technique drill goes in, the last stage becomes a penalty shootout, and the score card between drills becomes a 3-2-1.

Shop, Style and BootsBuilding now

Shop tiles reordered, Style redesigned with real names and pictures per level, fame always shown, a Holiday group and “My stuff”, Boots as a shop shelf.

Sponsors, Home and StatsBuilt · in test

Built in the test copy. Seen in a browser at 390 px and 360 px; no sideways scroll.

  • Sponsors strip on HomeseenOne tap opens Sponsors. Shows “1 offer waiting” with a red badge, your deals and weekly money, or how close your first deal is.
  • Progress bars instead of exact fameseen“Needs Icon (80 fame)” is now “Needs Icon” with a gold bar. The empty Deals text no longer says “(10 fame)”.
  • “Deal slots · UNKNOWN, 0 of 0 used” fixedseenMikey's rule gives a new career 0 slots (Harry, 1 Oct: keep it 0). It now reads “Your first slot opens at Local Name” with a bar.
  • Relations sponsors row was out of dateseenIt read the old sponsor list, so after signing a deal it still said “No deals signed yet”. It now shows your brand deals and waiting offers.
  • Energy and cans in one short cardseen303 px → about 130 px. Home is 148 px shorter overall. On a phone the cans still sit just below the first screen (the player card and next match fill it).
  • Stats tab: your season firstseenYour season stats come first, the league table underneath. (The recording was garbled here; this is the best reading.)

Real-match bugsBuilding now

The defender who intercepts from the wrong side, and the assist you didn't make.

  • Assist you didn't make: fixedmeasuredAny team-mate goal after your pass counted as your assist, even if someone else played it in between. Now the assist goes to the last team-mate to touch it. Wrongly credited: 14% → 0; with an ordered lay-off: 75% → 0.
  • Wrong-side interception: explained, now being fixed (your pick a + b)seen + measuredIn the Premier League the nearest defender runs straight at the ball the moment you pull back — through your body if he's behind you. His run goes through you in 6.8% of chances, 47% of one-on-ones. Building: (a) he goes round you, (b) from behind he fouls more, sometimes a penalty.
