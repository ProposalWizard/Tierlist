The detail

Your rule (1 Oct): never two levels from one match. Levels 11→20 cost 108,000 points (you said about 100,000); 81→90 cost 9.9 million (about 10 million). A test fails if any match ever gives two levels. (measured)

Trial: technique + shootoutBuilt · in test

Four stages now: Free kicks → Through the gate → Take him on → Penalty shootout. Five-a-side and find-the-pass are out, and the score card between stages is a 3-2-1.

Stage order: 5 stages → 4

Before: penalties, free kicks, dribble, find the pass, five-a-side. After: free kicks, the gate (technique), take him on, penalty shootout. Free kicks go first because the gate is the same strike with nobody in goal.

3

Penalty shootout: you take all your side's kicks

Five each, then sudden death. Their kicks are automatic, at your keeper. Both keepers get sharper each kick. Measured over 500 kicks per row: with only their keeper ramping you'd win 13% of shootouts; with both ramping, 47–50%. About 10 kicks per shootout, 25% go to sudden death. Your mark is the share of your kicks that go in, win or lose.

Look forAfter: your kick (saved here) then their kick (they score) with the shootout scoreboard at the top and 'Next: You / Next: Santos'. Before the finale was a five-a-side match; the old Penalties stage (3 kicks) is added as an extra clip.
