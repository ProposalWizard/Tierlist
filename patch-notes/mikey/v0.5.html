The headline: how you get picked

  • Win your shirt: a better-rated rival starts ahead of you until you earn it

    Problem: the other players at your club never counted. A brand-new 18-year-old started every game from week one, at Man City the same as at Barnet.

    Why: the manager only looked at you: his opinion of you, your form, your star rating and your fitness.

    Fix: your rival is the best real team-mate in your position.

    • Rated higher than him (signed as the main man): you start, on your own form.
    • Rated the same or lower: you start on the bench. The pre-match screen names him and compares your forms. You take his place with 2+ appearances at a recent average of 6.8 (about a goal and an assist across three sub appearances), or when he hits a bad patch (under 6.0) while you're at 6.3+.
    • Once won, ratings stop mattering. You keep your place on form, fitness and the manager, however much higher rated he is.
    • A new club means winning it again. Old saves where you've been playing keep your place. A made-up squad (no ratings) has no rival.

    measured not yet seen

    Why 6.8 and not 7.0

    A short sub appearance pulls your rating towards 6.5: a 25-minute goal is about 7.4, an assist about 7.0, nothing about 6.2. A 7.0 average would need two goals in three appearances.

Changed

  • Form counts faster
    The manager judges your last 3 matches (was 5), and form counts for 35% of his score (was 30%; his opinion of you 45% → 40%). Three poor games (5.5) now bench you, and three good ones off the bench (7.2) win your place back. Two dreadful games (4.8) are enough on their own. measured
  • A substitute comes on when the game needs him
    It was always somewhere between 58' and 72'. Now, from the 50th minute: 2 down 50' · 1 down 56' · level 64' · 1 up 72' · well ahead 80', plus up to 4 minutes either way. measured not yet seen
  • Cup rotation
    A regular is never rotated out. A player who isn't one starts about 60% of early cup rounds (anything before the quarter-final). measured
