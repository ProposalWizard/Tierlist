Fixed

  • Careers stuck after season 5: End of Season did nothing
    Found on Leo's save (Arsenal, season 5). Pressing End of Season crashed silently while building next season's Champions League draw, so the season never rolled over. Any save with an older Rule Book could hit this. measured
    Why it happened

    His save stored a UEFA Rule Book from before the "custom clubs in Europe" rule existed, so that rule was missing and the draw crashed reading it. Saved Rule Books now get any newer rules filled in with defaults. The browser console error Leo sent pointed straight to it. ruleBook.ts

Added

  • Fame levels
    Fame runs 0–100 across six levels: Unknown, Local Name 10, Rising Star 25, National Name 40, Global Star 60, Icon 80. Each level unlocks more sponsors. seen
    How you earn and lose it
    • Playing well earns nothing on its own. Big moments do: promotion +3 to +10, first time in Europe +5, trophies (league title +12, FA Cup +8, Champions League +15), Player of the Season +4, Young Player +3, Golden Boot +3, Team of the Season +2, Player of the Month +1, Ballon d'Or top three +5, win +15.
    • What you own adds fame too, with each item worth less the more you already own. The whole current shop is worth about 33.
    • Scandals and getting caught add 1–4 fame (it's a news story), but cost reputation.
    • You lose fame for relegation (−5), a season mostly on the bench (−4), and by fading back toward your division's normal level each season (National League 15 … Premier League 75).
  • Items wear out
    Phone about 2 seasons, cars 5, property and jewellery never. A worn-out item gives no fame and shows "WORN OUT — REPLACE" in the shop. seen
  • Fame & Reputation screen
    Ownership → Reputation now shows your fame level, your reputation, what each unlocks, and why both moved last season. seen
  • Buy more / Sell some in Portfolio
    "Sell entire stake" is gone. Buy more (up to 100%, can make you majority owner) or sell any amount with a slider. Every buy and sell now asks you to confirm, and warns if you're about to gain or lose majority. seen

Changed

  • Premier League wages go much higher at the top
    Now depends on the club's real wage bill, trophies won last season and your star rating, capped at ★100,000 a week. Other divisions unchanged. measured
    Coventry bench★2,534

    The three multipliers
    • Club spending power: real 2025-26 wage bills, Liverpool 13.5× Coventry, softened to 3.7× so squad players don't out-earn stars elsewhere.
    • Honours: league won last season ×1.2, Champions League ×1.3, any Ballon d'Or ×1.5.
    • Star rating: no effect below 4★, up to ×1.5 at 5★.
  • Reputation is one number
    0–100, starting at 20. 30+ boards take your recommendations seriously, 60+ you can propose Rule Book changes, 90+ you can run for president. Merging clubs now costs 20. Old saves take the average of the four old bars. seen
  • Sponsors must be earned again
    Deals last one season (two for Watch, Jewellery and Car), then you have to re-qualify. Unlocks follow fame levels; the luxury brands now want a car or a property you own instead of lifestyle points. measured
  • KIB cans: three cans, priced off your wage
    The three Stat Cans are removed. Energy cans now cost half a week (Basic), one week (Premium) and two weeks (Elite) of your wage, so they always cost something real. seen
  • Cheaper first boots, shorter boot life in the lower tiers
    NS-Pure ★725 → ★365. Starter, Semi-Pro and Pro boots last about half as long. NS-Swerve lasts 15 matches. seen
    NS-Pure70 35

  • A career can last 50 seasons
    Forced retirement at age 40 (about 22 seasons max) is gone. The career now ends after season 50. You can still choose to retire from 33. measured
  • Smaller balance tweaks
    A league title now counts 10× a Community Shield toward your star rating (was about 6×). Dilemma fame rewards scaled down to +1–3 (an advert used to give up to +15, more than a league title). Country call-ups now come at fame 40 (was 45). measured
