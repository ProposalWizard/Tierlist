The longer you stay, the more likely someone sees you.

The number of bets stands for the time you spend in the casino. Each bet adds a little to the chance. Money won or lost does not change the chance. It only changes how bad the story is.

  1. You place at least one betNo bet, no risk. Walking in and out is safe.
  2. Start at 2%Somebody might see you there.
  3. Add 0.4% for each bet after the firstEvery bet is time spent at a table.
  4. Multiply by your fameAn Unknown is barely noticed. An Icon is recognised by everybody.
  5. Stop at 25%The cap. Never a certainty.
  6. Roll once when you leaveAt most one story a week.
  7. The story breaks on MondayIt lands in the post-match feed, so you half-expect it all week.

What the money does now

  • Money never changes the chance
    Only the number of bets and your fame do.
  • Money changes what the story says
    If a story comes out, the headline uses what happened that visit:
That visitThe headline
Lost 8+ weeks' wages"…loses a fortune at the tables"
Won 8+ weeks' wages"…has a lucky night at the casino"
Anything else"…spotted at the casino"

What a story costs you

The cost grows with each story in the same season. A big loss makes that one story one step worse. A big win makes it one step milder. Reputation, boss, fans and team-mates are the 0–100 bars you already have.

Step 1
A small story
  • Reputation −3 · Fans −2
  • Fame +1 (a news story makes you better known)
  • A post in the media feed.
Step 2
A bad story
  • Reputation −8 · Boss −6 · Fans −5
  • Sponsors: deals with a behaviour clause end, and the others become unhappy
  • The season is no longer "clean", so you lose the end-of-season reputation bonus
  • Fame +2
Step 3
"Problem gambler" exposé
  • Reputation −15 · Boss −12 · Team-mates −6
  • The club fines you 2 weeks' wages
  • You lose the captaincy, if you have it
  • Fame +3
Step 4 and after
The club steps in
  • All of step 3 again
  • The club bans you from the casino for 8 weeks (the doors are locked)

One extra idea: betting on football

  • Bets on who wins a competition are a real rule break
    The casino already takes bets on who wins the league and the cups. Real players are banned for this: Ivan Toney got 8 months in 2023. These bets could carry their own risk. For example, each bet has a 10% chance of an FA charge: a fine of 10 weeks' wages and a ban of 4–8 matches.

Ways to lower the risk (optional)

  • A private room
    A shop item that halves the chance. It costs a lot.
  • Lawyers
    The game already has lawyers for corruption. They could cut the cost of a story, but not stop it.

Already in the game today

  • A hidden version of this already exists
    Today, if you lose more than 8 weeks' wages in one visit, there is a 1 in 4 chance of a story. That story only upsets sponsors. There is no post, and no reputation, boss or fan cost. Nobody can see that it happened. This proposal would replace it.

What changed from v1
  • Money lost no longer adds to the chance (it added up to +12%). Bets now carry the whole chance: +0.4% each, was +0.25%.
  • A huge win no longer adds +3%.
  • Other visits in the last 4 weeks no longer add +2% each. Every bet already counts, so more visits already means more chance.
  • The 25% cap stays.
  • Money now picks the headline and moves the story one step up (a big loss) or down (a big win).
Where it goes in the game (for the builder)
  • The roll happens when you leave the casino: handleCasinoExit and handleCasinoBank in app/star-dev/page.tsx. That is where the hidden rule lives today.
  • The casino needs to count bets in each visit.
  • The costs reuse what exists: brandScandal (sponsors), lastScandalSeason (clean season), REPUTATION_EVENTS, scandalFame.
  • The story is a new media moment, so it shows in the post-match feed.
