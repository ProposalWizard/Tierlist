A mix of all of them, and never guaranteed.

One bet opens a small chance. More bets, bigger losses, a huge win and coming back often each add to it. The more famous you are, the more the press watch. The chance stops at 25%, so no single visit is ever a sure story.

  1. You place at least one betNo bet, no risk. Walking in and out is safe.
  2. Start at 2%Somebody might see you there.
  3. Add what you did that visitBets, money lost, a huge win, other visits this month.
  4. Multiply by your fameAn Unknown is barely noticed. An Icon is followed everywhere.
  5. Stop at 25%The cap. Never a certainty.
  6. Roll once when you leaveAt most one story a week.
  7. The story breaks on MondayIt lands in the post-match feed, so you half-expect it all week.

Why not time spent, or money spent?
  • Time spent: a phone left open on the casino screen is not gambling. Counting time would punish someone who walked away from their phone. The number of bets measures the same thing fairly.
  • Money spent (total staked): this mostly repeats the number of bets. The money you lose is what makes a story, so that is what counts.
  • Money won: only a huge win counts, and it adds less than a loss. "Star wins big" is a story, but a milder one.

What a story costs you

Each story in the same season is worse than the last. Reputation, boss and fans are the 0–100 bars you already have.

1st story
"Spotted at the casino"
  • Reputation −3 · Fans −2
  • Fame +1 (a news story makes you better known)
  • A post in the media feed with a photo-style card.
2nd story, or any night you lost 8+ weeks' wages
"Heavy night at the tables"
  • Reputation −8 · Boss −6 · Fans −5
  • Sponsors: deals with a behaviour clause end, and the others become unhappy
  • The season is no longer "clean", so you lose the end-of-season reputation bonus
  • Fame +2
3rd story
"Problem gambler" exposé
  • Reputation −15 · Boss −12 · Team-mates −6
  • The club fines you 2 weeks' wages
  • You lose the captaincy, if you have it
  • Fame +3
4th story and after
The club steps in
  • All of step 3 again
  • The club bans you from the casino for 8 weeks (the doors are locked)
A huge-win story
"Lucky night for…"
  • Reputation −2
  • Fame +2
  • It still counts as a story, so the next one is a step worse.

The ladder starts again each season. The numbers match how the game already scores a scandal: getting caught in corruption costs reputation −15, boss −10 and fans −8.

One extra idea: betting on football

  • Bets on who wins a competition are a real rule break
    The casino already takes bets on who wins the league and the cups. Real players are banned for this: Ivan Toney got 8 months in 2023. These bets could carry their own risk. For example, each bet has a 10% chance of an FA charge: a fine of 10 weeks' wages and a ban of 4–8 matches.
  • Betting against your own club
    This would be the worst case: double the chance, and a longer ban.

Ways to lower the risk (optional)

  • A private room
    A shop item that halves the chance. It costs a lot.
  • Lawyers
    The game already has lawyers for corruption. They could cut the cost of a story, but not stop it.

Already in the game today

  • A hidden version of this already exists
    Today, if you lose more than 8 weeks' wages in one visit, there is a 1 in 4 chance of a story. That story only upsets sponsors. There is no post, and no reputation, boss or fan cost. Nobody can see that it happened. This proposal would replace it.
