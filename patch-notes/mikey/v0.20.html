    The details

    Settings → first card, next to Sound effects. Off on every phone until switched on. A track only downloads once the switch is on: 11 files, about 6 MB in total.

    Each loop's end runs into its start: the sound either side of every join matches 97 to 99.9%, measured on the finished files.

    Recordings: VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition, CC0 (free for any use, no credit needed). Mikey's Blue Lock files are not in the game or the code.

    lib/star/gameMusic.ts picks the track; public/star/music/ holds the files; tools/game-music/ remakes them. The match file (sensitive) only reads the score, clock and screen for this; nothing the engine gets is changed.
