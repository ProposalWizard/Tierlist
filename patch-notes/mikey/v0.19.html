Fixed

  • Team-mates' goals were wiped on every reload
    At a club with no real players, every reload replaced the squad, so team-mates showed 5 goals between 4 players against your 40. Now the squad and its tallies are kept. measured
  • PL Draft FC 27 offered 177 clubs
    The live club list for FC 27 had AC Milan, Real Madrid, Accrington and junk names like "espa". It now has the game's 20. Bad two-digit years (26, 22, 19) are gone too. measured on the live answer
  • Player search failed for common names
    Settings → Dev → Squad: "kane" returned an error (database time limit). Now it finds Harry Kane in about 1 s, and accented names work (Ødegaard, Müller, Mbappé). measured
  • The build would have stopped every deploy
    The corner rule below flew the ball outside the match engine, which the one-engine guard blocks. The measurement now lives in the engine as a read-only helper. Engine behaviour is unchanged (finishing, aiming and outcomes tests pass). Production build passes. measured

Changed

  • Your player is shown on every chance
    He was hidden on shooting chances (Harry's 12 Oct call). He now stands on every chance, moved clear of the ball and the aim arrow when the chance opens. To be agreed with Harry. not yet seen
  • Corners only if you can reach the box
    Your best corner must carry 34.4 m in the air to the middle of the box, measured with the real ball. Today that is about power 60. If power is retuned, the line moves by itself. measured
