Added

  • Energy modes during a match
    An energy bar with Low / Medium / High buttons now sits at the bottom of the commentary screen, where the repeated stats row used to be. High uses energy faster and gets you about 35% more chances; Low uses half as much and gets you about 35% fewer. Medium plays exactly like before. seen
    Low30
    Medium60
    High95
    Measured in a real match

    Premier League: Medium drained 0.667 a minute (100 → 64 by minute 54), High 1.075 a minute. Both match the plan.

  • A real match clock
    The minute now ticks up one at a time (1, 2, 3 …) instead of jumping between commentary lines, and your energy falls with it. About 0.7 seconds per minute at normal speed. seen
  • KIB can at half time
    A Use KIB can button sits beside Second Half, showing how many Basic cans you have. Each tap gives +25 energy. Cans come off your stock once the match is saved. seen
  • Rest days
    Every day between matches gives +8 energy (Saturday to Saturday is 6 days, Saturday to Wednesday is 3). Owning a property adds a little, and so does your club's training ground. measured

Changed

  • Unused weekly actions no longer give energy
    Each one used to be worth +20 when you played, so a week of doing nothing gained you 28. That's gone; rest days replace it. measured
  • Bigger games cost more energy
    From the Premier League down to the National League (78% of the cost). FA Cup, League Cup and Community Shield cost the opponent's league. Europa League is 6% more, Champions League and Super Cup 11% more. measured
  • Being tired matters more
    Bench below 65 (was 35), out of the squad below 40 (was 15). Power and curve up to 30% weaker when tired (was 15%). Tired-legs subs below 25, extra injury risk below 30. Tiredness no longer changes how many chances you get; the mode does. seen
  • Training and Rest
    Training costs 30 energy (was 15). Rest gives +10 (was +20). All of these values can be changed in the tuning editor. measured

Known issues

  • Switching mode changes chances a little late Low
    Energy use changes from the very next minute, but chances only change from the next stretch of play, usually a few match minutes later. Making it instant means rebuilding how the hidden match runs.
  • Production build not run for this version Low
    The PC was out of memory. Type-check and every test pass; the build should be run once memory is free.
  • Low mode's exact drain not measured on screen Low
    It was seen draining slower than Medium, but not timed precisely. Tests confirm the rate.
