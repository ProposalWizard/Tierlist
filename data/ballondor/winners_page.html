That year, per the site

  • Awarded on 18 December 1956, just ahead of Alfredo Di Stéfano; presented at a civic reception at Blackpool Town Hall.
  • Aged 41 years, 10 months and 17 days – still the oldest winner (men and women).
  • Season before the award: led Blackpool to their highest league finish in 25 years, runners-up to Manchester United's 'Busby Babes'.
  • No team title in the calendar year – the first winner to win without one.
  • Played on until 50, retiring on 6 February 1965 with Stoke City (3–1 v Fulham).
