Where the site disagrees with itself

  • 1994: the Stoichkov article says he "narrowly beat" his rival. The 1994 points in another article are 210 for Stoichkov and 136 for Roberto Baggio.
  • 2019: the closest-margins article puts Messi 686, Van Dijk 679 "two years earlier" than 2022. The figures sit in its 2019 section.
  • 2025: Dembélé's goals: 37 in the France Football interview box, 35 in the "13 players" article.
  • Dembélé's year: one article says he was "crowned the 2026 men's champion". Every ranking page shows 2025.
How this was put together
  • Opened ballondor.com/winners in a real browser (the site blocks plain downloads).
  • Read the ranking page for every year, 1956–2025.
  • Read all 47 winners' player pages.
  • Read about 155 articles from the site's article lists, and kept only facts about a winner's own award year.
  • Points, ages and records appear only where an article states them.
