Fixed

  • Scoring was halved. Now it isn't.
    Four separate bugs, each found by measuring.
    Cutback
    41.3% 9.8
    One-on-one
    47.6% 9.8
    Tight angle
    39.7% 13.3
    Header
    26.8% 6.6
    Through ball
    18.7% 11.2
    What the four bugs were
    • The keeper was never beaten. A one-on-one is a chance about the keeper, and his tuned positioning had been overwritten. 9.8% conversion with 0% blocked — nothing in the way, he just always saved it.
    • Cutbacks came from the touchline — 20.2m off centre, where the real game puts them at 11.5m.
    • The "is he blocking?" test was blind. Of 300 cutbacks it found 0 blockers. 223 had a man within 1.5m of the ball's real path.
    • Two rules cancelled each other — one filled the middle, one cleared the shooting lane, in the wrong order.

    One-on-one is above its old number on purpose: the old one had a defender in the way 99.8% of the time, so it was never really a one-on-one.

  • The goal stopped changing size
    One zoom band, goal in the same spot every chance. 69% of chances used to be framed wrong.
    Three causes
    • The builder had a free zoom slider with a 5× range.
    • The generator used three different heights — 69.2% weren't the standard one.
    • The frame secretly grew to keep the keeper in shot — another 10%.

    Zooming out doesn't help: a long shot framed wider is emptier, 75% grass vs 60.9%.

  • The camera stopped moving the players
    It was physically dragging defenders into frame, so zooming in squashed the defence and changed the football.
    What it was breaking
    • Passes found a man 97 times in 220.
    • A defender a test deliberately planted in the shooting path was being moved by the camera instead.

    Both normal again, nothing re-tuned. Now: ball, you, keeper and every runner in frame 100%. Only defenders drift off — 1.44 per chance. Corners and crosses exempt, because you're delivering into that box.

    To revert: CAMERA_MOVES_PLAYERS = true and VIEW_MIN_H = 42 at the top of canvasEngine.ts. Tests pass either way.

  • Chances now match their own name
    One-on-ones broken 99.7% → 0%. Corners 19.7% → 0%. Byline crosses 8.7% → 0%.
  • Defenders stopped hiding near their own keeper on long shots
    The back line now holds the edge of the box. Was 10.4–13.6m out, now 13.8–16.1m.

  • "The goal wasn't on screen" — not the camera
    Goal on screen 100% of the time on all 11 shooting types. It's 0% on build-up and midfield passes, by design. Same bug as too much build-up.

    Not auto-fixed on purpose — a wrong repair rule is worse than none. We proved that when a bad rule flagged 32.2% of pictures as broken and the real figure was 0.64%.

Next — live scenarios

    Drag a line from your player, a teammate or an opponent to set what happens when the chance starts.
  • When you release the ball
    A real fork — pick one deliberately.
    The two options
    • Manual: drag your run, choose the moment to play it. More control, more skill, more to learn.
    • Auto-dribble: your player carries it in a direction, you only pick the shot or pass. Simpler, closer to now.

Next — everything else

  • Rule sets per chance type blocked on Harry
    You build the perfect bases, the rules get read off them, the game generates inside those rules.
    Why it's the key to formations

    A draft read off the current one-on-ones already found something: only 96.2% have the ball ahead of every defender. Should be 100% — that's the definition.

    "Ball 8–16m out" is a fixed box and breaks when a formation moves the defence. "Ball ahead of every defender" stays true wherever they stand. That's why a 3-5-2 must not put three defenders in front of a one-on-one: the chance's own rules outrank the formation.

  • Talk to Claude from inside a scenario
    A comment box on the exact picture that reaches me as a real instruction.
    Why this one matters

    An automatic checker only catches rules somebody already wrote down. Every real bug on this project so far has been a rule nobody had written yet. A box where you type "the defender shouldn't be there" on the picture is how that gap closes.
