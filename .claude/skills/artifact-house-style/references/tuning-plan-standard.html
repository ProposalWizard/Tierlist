PLAN

Tune the match by playing it

You play and say what feels wrong. We turn that into the numbers. Defenders get smarter as you go up the leagues.

1 · TUNING, NOT A TARGET TABLE

You don't set numbers. You play matches and tell us in plain words — "not enough chances", "too many cutbacks", "I never get the ball on my wing". We find the cause, change it, and you play again.
The numbers stay behind the scenes, so every fix is measured, not guessed. Anyone who wants them can open them.

2 · THE TUNER: HOW A SENTENCE BECOMES A FIX

One thing you say often has more than one cause. The tuner plays hundreds of matches in seconds, tries each possible cause, and keeps the smallest change that fixes it without breaking anything else.
It always shows you before and after, and nothing changes until you say yes. Real example from today: the match "gives up" and hands you a chance when you've waited too long — and that fallback ignores which wing you play on. That's why wingers rarely get their own wing.

3 · DEFENDERS GET A BRAIN, BY LEAGUE

Today every defender reacts the same, instantly, and the back line moves as one block. Real defenders make mistakes — more of them lower down. A National League centre-back steps up late; a Premier League line holds.
StageWhat defenders learn

Each stage can go live on its own. Better players (by their real rating) make fewer mistakes, so a strong National League side still defends well. Your scoring stays the same unless you choose to make a league harder.
