Fixed

  • The keeper's own camera showed a giant head, not a goal
    Caught live, on the first ship: posts projected thousands of px off both edges, the grass vanished, the keeper was reduced to one giant head.
    What the real fix was
    • Computed the camera's real pixel output across a sweep of candidate distances, instead of more hand algebra.
    • CAM_Y=7m puts both posts in frame with real margin.
    • The ball itself was drawn at literal physical scale — a few px, invisible. Fixed the same way tiny sprites already are elsewhere: draw it oversized on purpose.
  • On phone, touching the screen instantly dove — no way to aim first
    Desktop could hover the mouse to preview a save; touch had no equivalent, so every tap was a blind commit.
    The fix, to your own spec
    • Press and hold now only starts tracking — nothing commits yet.
    • Dragging updates a live reticle the same way for mouse-hover and touch-drag.
    • Releasing commits the dive at wherever you let go.
    • A plain click with no drag still commits immediately — desktop unchanged.

Added

  • Goalie Mode — a new keeper reflex minigame, in the Casino
    Camera facing the shot, drag-to-aim (see Fixed), a bet that stacks per consecutive save, and a dive that physically takes time — so diving right as the strike happens has to be the winning play, not holding your gloves somewhere and hoping.
    How the timing was tuned

    The core tension is measured, not assumed: committing right at the strike saves 90–100% on both difficulties. Committing early — before the shot even starts — saves only ~28%, barely above the 12% floor of never diving at all. The same early timing that's still fine at streak 0 becomes a guaranteed miss by streak 20, as the window tightens with the multiplier.

  • A real stadium behind the goal
    Curved roof, two floodlight towers, a speckled crowd, hoarding, a running-track stripe — replacing the flat sky.

Changed

  • The camera now pushes in, then pulls back — like the reference you sent
    Three eased stages: a wide establishing shot, a tight personal push-in right up to the strike, then a hard pull back to wide for the save itself.

    A real bug caught by computing it, not by shipping and finding out

    The tight push-in that matches the reference would, on its own, have put a full-width dive almost two screens off to the side — checked directly against the keeper's own real max reach through the actual projection math, before it ever shipped. Fixed by pulling back to a wide shot in time for the dive to land fully in frame, reusing the exact projection constants already proven in the first camera fix.

    Extracted real frames from your three videos to build this (PyAV — no ffmpeg/cv2 in the sandbox) and read them with a precise 10% grid overlay, not by eye alone.
