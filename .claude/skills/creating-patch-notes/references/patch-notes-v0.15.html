Headline · what we discussed

Gameplay, measured against real football

Problem

The keeper, shots, the ball, the match between your chances and dribbling didn't behave like football: keepers saved as well from 5 m as from 25 m, the match you don't play had no corners, and dribbling didn't need timing.

Why

Each was tuned by feel, never checked against real numbers.

Fix

Each job took real figures first (a full Premier League season of StatsBomb data for keepers, published season averages, FIFA ball tests), measured the game over 1,000s of runs, then fixed the gaps. Old is always one tap away in Settings → Look.

Honest: the numbers are measured; most of it has not yet been seen on a phone. Try it and point at what feels wrong.

Problems fixed

The scoreboard and the stats screen disagreed
Problem

Get taken off late and the stats screen showed one more goal for the other side than the scoreboard (France 2-0 on the board, 3-0 after).

Why

When someone else took a free kick or penalty, the match ran on in pieces, and only the last piece's commentary was kept. The score counted the lost goals; the scoreboard didn't.

Fix

Every piece's lines are kept. In 1,500 test matches: 88 wrong → 0.

The camera showed a lot of empty pitch
Problem

Harry: “the camera view shows a huge area of the pitch where no one's at.”

Why

The camera kept every man on screen, so one player far away set the zoom.

Fix

It frames the men the chance is about (ball, you, the pass target, close defenders, keeper). Everyone else is wholly on screen or clearly off, never cut in half. Settings → Look → Match camera: New | Old.

Added and changed

Keeper pro: a goalkeeper built from real data
Real data

All 380 matches of a Premier League season (StatsBomb): 71% of shots on target saved, 43% from under 8 m, 92% from 28 m+. He holds 40–48% of saves.

Now

He stands where real keepers stand, saves where the ball is, holds or parries by pace and height, parries go wide, he comes for about 1 in 8 corners, and a 90 keeper beats a 60.

Switch

Settings → Look → Gameplay → Keeper pro: New | Old.

Shots: where you hit the ball decides the height
Problem

A middle tap often flew at the bar (12% of penalties went over), any tap a bit off-centre curled, and there was almost no spread off the arrow.

Now

Top of the ball = low, middle = hip height, below = top corner, bottom = chip. The middle fifth goes straight. Real spread, shown as a faint cone on the arrow (87 in 100 land inside). Contact grades: CLEAN, SLICED, TOPPED, SCUFFED.

Switch

Settings → Look → Gameplay → Shots: New | Old.

The real match engine, a penalty mid-drag: the faint cone beside the arrow.

Penalties scored (real 76%)48%68%
One-on-ones scored (real 30–40%)40%41%

A thumb bot with human-like error, 500 chances a kind. The plan's “68% one-on-ones” was with perfect aim; with a real thumb it was already about 40%.

Knuckleball and ⚡ power shot, in the real match
Knuckleball

A hard pull (80%+) tapped dead centre: no spin, it wobbles late.

Power shot

Tap ⚡ on the aim screen. Let go, a 0.45 s wind-up while defenders close, then hit the green circle fast for up to 1.5× pace.

Switch

Settings → Look → Gameplay → Knuckleball & power shot. Not seen on a screen yet.

Dribble duels: timing beats a man
Problem

You didn't have to time anything: random swiping won 43%, slow timing 36%, pace changed nothing.

Now

Each defender closes, jockeys and shows you one way, plants (the tell), then tackles or fouls. Tap a side = drop the shoulder, swipe down = step-over, flick = knock it past. Dribbling and pace stats matter.

Switch

Settings → Look → Dribble duels: New | Old.

Ball physics (testing, off by default)
What

A real football: air drag, a grass bounce and roll, spin that dies away, round posts, a net that catches it, the same result at any frame rate.

Measured

A goal kick carried 70 m (real 54 m), now 54 m. A 2 m drop bounced 0.97 m (real 0.60–0.85), now 0.67 m. Woodwork hits 1.7% → 2.9% of shots (real about 3%).

Why off

With it on, 3 calibrated match tests fail and nobody has seen it on a screen. Settings → Look → Ball physics: New to try.
