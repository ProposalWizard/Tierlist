Headline

One last game: the farewell match

Problem

A 20-season career ended on a screen. There was no last game and no goodbye.

Why

Nothing sat between "The final whistle" and the end screen. The game also kept no record of who you played with.

Fix

After Hang them up, your last club invites you to one last game on the real match:

  • Your XI, named after you ("Calloway XI"): your best team-mates from every club. You play your own position.
  • Rivals XI: every man who beat you to a Ballon d'Or starts. The rest are the best players at the clubs that beat you to titles.
  • The sides stay within 6 rating points of each other, so it is a real game.
The order
  • The invite: Play it or Skip.
  • The team sheets.
  • A guard of honour: both teams clap you out of the tunnel (3D, or a drawn version on phones without 3D).
  • The match. At 85' you come off, and the whole ground stands for you. The match plays on to full time.
  • Full time: the score, who scored, your goals, assists and rating.
  • Hang them up: the career overview, now with a gold Farewell strip.

Where Your XI comes from
  • From today, every season keeps your 5 best team-mates (step 0 on the plans page).
  • They are ranked by rating, plus seasons together, plus goals and assists. The team-mate you played with longest always makes the squad.
  • A career from before today has none saved for its early seasons. The XI then uses the squads still kept for your old clubs, then your last club's dressing room.
  • Every side gets keepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards, so nobody plays in goal by accident.
What this means for you: retire, then play one last game with your favourite team-mates against the men who beat you. Skip it if you want; the career goes into the Hall of Fame either way.

Your records live on
Asked for

"Every new career gets your old bests to chase."

Fix

The bests of your retired careers become records in your later careers. There are nine: most goals in a season, most assists in a season, most goals in a game, most career goals, most career assists, most appearances, most trophies, most Ballons d'Or and the furthest goal.

  • Stats → Records has a new card, Hall of Fame records: who holds each one, the number, the season, and a bar for how close you are.
  • Home shows one line when you get close: "3 goals off Calloway's record". When level: "one more beats it".
  • Breaking one puts news posts on your phone, once, naming the old holder.
  • Equalling a record never takes it. The first to set it keeps it.

Seen in the real game on a save made for it: two retired careers in the Hall, a third at 48 goals.

Share picture: a whole career in one image
Asked for

"One picture of a whole career for WhatsApp or Instagram."

Fix

Share picture on the end screen and on every Hall of Fame career. One picture, 1080 × 1350: your name, the legacy score, five big numbers, your clubs, goals by season and the cabinet. On a phone, Share opens WhatsApp, Instagram and the rest. On a computer, Save picture.

Share a career by code
Asked for

"Online: share a career, compare with a friend. Friends first."

Fix

Any Hall of Fame career can get a link like knowitball.co.uk/legend/K7Q2XM.

  • Anyone with the link sees the whole career overview. No sign-in to look; you sign in to make a link.
  • The same career always gets the same code. Stop sharing turns the link off.
  • A code is 6 letters and numbers with no look-alikes (no I, O, 0 or 1): about a billion codes.
  • No public board. Nothing lists the shared careers; you see one only if you have its link.
Compare with a friend
Fix

Head to head: two careers, 11 numbers, two bars each, and a count of numbers won. Clubs is shown but not counted: more clubs is not better or worse.

  • From a friend's link: Compare, then pick one of your careers.
  • From the Hall of Fame: Compare, paste their code or link, then pick one of yours.

Fixed (found by the playtest)

  • Free kicks and corners in the farewell went to team-mates
    The match picks a set-piece taker by position, and the farewell never told it otherwise: a striker took 3 free kicks in 8 and 1 corner in 4. The playtest got only 2 or 3 chances a match. My first check left position out, so it measured 7.3 chances that the game never gave. Now every set piece is yours and your side makes extra chances: a striker's chances by 85' went from 6.3 (normal) to 8.9 (measured).
  • Every farewell was the same match
    Five played farewells had the same team-mate chances at 18' and 19', the same rival goal at 84', and all ended 0–1. Every career that ended in season 20 got one fixed match script. Now each career gets its own.
  • A Ballon d'Or rival's surname on both sides
    "Williams" and "Modric" were on both team sheets in every run. A Ballon d'Or rival is always picked, so the surname rule skipped him. Now your team-mate with that surname sits out. Over 15 test careers: 3 clashes before, 0 now.
  • The ovation came before the commentary reached 85'
    The banner showed while the clock still read 75'. The banner and the cheer now wait for the clock, and the banner says the same words as the commentary.
  • The Back button covered the team-sheet label
    "Farewell match · Aldershot" ran under it. The label is now "Farewell match".
  • The guard of honour took 40 to 80 seconds on a slow device
    Its clock moved at most 0.05 seconds a frame. It now runs in real time down to 4 frames a second, so it lasts about 10 seconds.
  • "1 trophies"
    Now "1 trophy" on the end screen and in the share text.

Fixed (found while checking)

  • The same surname on both team sheets
    A Taylor, a Firmino and a Werner played for both sides in the made-up careers. Now a surname appears once across both sides (a Ballon d'Or rival is always picked). With the rule off: 24 clashes over 15 test careers. With it on: 0.
  • The Hall copy of a career drew a different overview
    After step 0, 12 Hall checks failed: the overview carried the saved team-mates. The overview leaves them out now. All pass.
  • The guard of honour could not read Harry's smaller 3D files
    His update packs the 3D files smaller and changes how scenes pick their quality. The walk-out now uses both. Seen: it loads in 5 to 15 seconds on this test machine, which has no graphics chip.

Known issues

One database file to run

supabase/migrations/star_legend_shares.sql, in the Supabase SQL Editor. Until then Share link says "Sharing isn't switched on yet." Nothing else breaks. Still waiting too: star_hall_of_fame.sql (the Hall in the cloud).

  • Retiring still ends the save
    Legacy points and playing on as your son are yours to build later, as you said.
  • Sharing and comparing are not seen working end to end
    The test machine cannot reach the database. The screens, the codes and the compare maths are tested; the save and fetch are not.
  • The farewell is one go
    Skip it and it is gone. Play it and the score stays on the career. A refresh during it goes back to the final whistle, so you can start it again.
  • The Old UI has no farewell, share picture or compare
    It is frozen. Its retired careers still go in the Hall and still set records.
  • Signed out on a test machine, a refresh during the farewell goes back to the Ballon d'Or night
    The older test-mode bug from v0.7. Signed-in players go back to the final whistle (worked out from the code).
  • The world does not age carried from v0.7
    Other clubs' players never get older or retire. It matters for long careers and for the son idea later.
