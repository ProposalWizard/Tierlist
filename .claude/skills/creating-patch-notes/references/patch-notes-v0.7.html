Headline

Retiring never deletes a career any more: the Hall of Fame

Problem

The end screen's only button was "Start a new career". It deleted the retired career, on the phone and in the cloud. Twenty seasons, gone.

Why

A career lived only in its save slot. Starting again in that slot had to clear it.

Fix

Every retired career is copied into a Hall of Fame the moment it is saved. It is a slim copy: everything the career overview shows, without the other clubs' squads or the phone's feed.

  • The title screen has a gold Hall of Fame button with a count.
  • Tap a career: its whole overview, all seven pages. Remove asks first.
  • A career that retired before today goes in the next time the title screen opens.
  • Before New career, New game or Delete can replace a retired career, the game checks it is in the Hall. If the phone is full, it asks first.

Seen in a browser, in the real game, signed out: after New career, New game and Delete, the Hall entry was byte-for-byte the same. The overview drawn from a Hall copy matched the full career for 11 test careers (measured).

Remove asks first
What this means for you: finish a career and start another; the old one is one tap away on the title screen. On this phone for now: it reaches your account on every device once one database file is run (Known issues).

  • The final-season warning and the final whistle sat in the top half of the screen
    They are now in the middle on a tall phone, and still fit a short one (390 × 664) without scrolling.

Fixed (part 1, earlier today)

  • The last season's Ballon d'Or, Golden Boot and Player of the Season were never counted
    Retiring skipped the end-of-season step that counts them. It now runs it. Seen: a test career that won the Ballon d'Or in its last season showed "1× Ballon d'Or".
  • A season's row named the wrong club after a summer move
    Moving at the end of a season made that season's history describe the new club (finish, wage, money). It now records the club you played for. Five new checks fail on the old code.
  • Who won what is recorded every season
    Every winner, the Ballon d'Or top three and your own place, from now on. It fills the overview's "Who won" page. Older seasons stay blank: they were never recorded.

Known issues

One database file to run

supabase/migrations/star_hall_of_fame.sql, in the Supabase SQL Editor. Until then the Hall stays on the phone it was made on (it says "Kept on this device for now"). Nothing breaks without it.

  • The world does not age found, not fixed
    Other clubs' players never get older or retire: only your own age goes up. After 20 seasons the squads are the same people at the same ages, apart from transfers. Read in the code, not seen in a long game. It is question 2 on the plans page.
  • A save already past season 20 retires at the end of the season it is in
    Only saves from before this change (the old rules let you play on). It gets the final whistle, but no warning first.
  • The Old UI has no warning screen and no Hall button
    The Old UI is frozen. It follows the 20-season rule (at season 20 its old screen says "That is the end of it") and its retired careers still go in the Hall; you see them from the New UI's title screen.
  • Signed out on a test machine, a refresh on the Ballon d'Or night can land on Home
    Carried from part 1. Test mode only; signed-in saves are not affected (read in the code).
  • Outside the Premier League, the Champions League winner can come from your own division
    Carried from part 1: about 1 season in 20 a Championship club "wins" it (worked out, not measured).
Small things the playtest noticed (not changed)
  • Offers in the last summer still say 2 years or more. The career ends after one.
  • No Season Awards screen after season 20. The awards still count; the final whistle comes straight after the Ballon d'Or night.
  • Home in season 20 has no "final season" mark.
  • All seasons counts the season being played as a season at the club; the old tables did not (7 against 6, mid-season).
Still open from v0.6 (not touched this round)
  • The power-shot wind-up has no leg pull-back (test match).
  • The chance after a dribble pass may play out too fast to read.
  • Dribble team-mates are plain blue shirts, and the pass is instant.
  • Some test screens get the easier free-kick keeper.
  • Some menu screens can be taller than the phone.

Closed since v0.6 by the others: the six database security holes (the file was run) and the test pages without a login check (locked in Harry's v0.29).
