Headline

Keepers are beatable now, and they can't see through people

Problem

Keepers were too good everywhere. A well-struck shot into the top corner beat a top keeper only 1 time in 4. Weak and strong keepers felt almost the same.

Why

Every keeper reached the top corner as easily as the bottom corner. A low-rated keeper only lost a little speed, and nothing about players standing in front of him made any difference.

Fix

Three changes, all in the keeper's decision-making, none in the core engine file (sensitive):

  • Top corners: a shot high and wide of where he stood shrinks his reach. Worse keepers lose more.
  • Worse keepers are worse: slower to react, slower to move and worse at reading the shot. A 95-rated keeper is exactly as before.
  • Goalie vision: bodies between the ball and the keeper make him react a little later, dive a little later and sometimes lean the wrong way first. It is capped small on purpose.

The detail
  • Bottom-corner shots did not change on purpose. The test showed 56% → 49% on the average keeper; that row is sampling noise, because the change never touches a low shot.
  • Long shots rose most: 27% → 34% against an average keeper. Harry's long-shot setting may want a look.
  • Penalties are left out of the top-corner change, which has its own rules (see trial penalties below).
  • Match free kicks are unchanged (9% for an ordinary taker, 19% for a specialist). Applying the top-corner cut to them doubled an ordinary taker to 19%, so it is off there.
  • Files: lib/star/keeperBrain.ts, lib/star/kindRules/freeKick.ts, lib/star/penaltyKeeper.ts. New test: tests/star/keeperHuman.mts.

Fixed

Trial free kicks and penalties were too hard
Problem

"Goalies far too good in free kicks and a bit too good in penalties."

Why

The trial's keeper read free kicks as quickly as a real match keeper. In penalties, a keeper who dived the right way always had his full reach.

Fix

Free kicks in the trial and in training get a slower keeper who sees, reacts and reads later. In trial penalties, a keeper who guesses right keeps 90% of his reach at a low rating, down to 78% at a high one. It was 100%.

Why the hard free kick is still only 3%

It is not the keeper. On the hardest free kicks, about 1 in 4 end "offside" because a team-mate standing offside touches the ball, and about 1 in 3 hit the wall. That comes from how the trial builds the picture. Fixing it is a separate job.

Five-a-side keepers ran like outfielders and never dived
Problem

Both keepers in five-a-side used the same running figure as everyone else. They never crouched, spread their hands or dived.

Why

That screen drew keepers with the outfield figure. The real match has a separate keeper pose; five-a-side never used it.

Fix

Both keepers now use the real match's keeper pose: set with hands out while waiting, thrown across when diving.

Seen in a browser: 40 match minutes, no freeze, no floating heads.

"Floating heads" in five-a-side

That bug was already fixed on the main game on 1 October. If you still see it, send a screenshot. It may be an older copy of the game, or a new case.

No way back from several screens
Problem

"Not always easy to go to home screen or back." The trial stages, the training drills and the scout, contract and offers screens had no exit at all.

Those screens were built as one-way steps with no button out.

The game scrolled during play half fixed
Problem

"Sometimes scrolling in game. The match screen should always fit the screen."

Why

Goalie Mode was a little taller than the screen. In a match, the pitch keeps at least its normal shape, so on a short phone (an iPhone 13 with the browser bars showing) it ends about 25–40 px below the bottom of the screen. The match then scrolls on purpose to bring the ball into view.

Fix

Goalie Mode now fits the screen and never scrolls (seen: page height 664 of 664). The match is left as it was. Locking it cut off the bottom of the pitch, and the ball can be there, which is worse than a small scroll. The real fix is a choice for you, below.

Added

Dribble runs: pass to a team-mate, and a bigger reward for going further
Problem

"Too simple, easy, repetitive." Also, beating 7 or more men was meant to give a chance near the penalty spot, but it never did.

Why

There was nothing to do but dodge. The reward was ignored because the even highlight mix (which deals out kinds of chance evenly) picked a random chance instead.

Fix
  • Three blue team-mates run ahead: one on each wing and one through the middle. Each has a ring on the grass: green easy, amber risky, red likely cut out. Tap one to pass at any moment.
  • The difficulty changes as you run, with distance, defenders in the lane and how square the ball is. Your vision nudges the odds. A pass that is cut out loses the ball.
  • The chance you get depends on how far you got. Passing early gives a long shot; beating every wave gives a one-on-one or cutback.
  • Defenders on the outside close you down harder, the last wave is tougher, and a match run is now 3–4 waves (was 2–4).

Knuckleball test match only
Asked for

The keeper guesses wrong more often than right and changes his mind as it flies. Two fingers with curve boots.

How

In /star-match-dev, open New shots and turn Curve boots on. Shoot, then swipe two fingers up the screen while the ball flies. A KNUCKLEBALL banner shows. The ball zig-zags late, up to about half a metre. The keeper leans one way, changes his mind, sometimes twitches again, and ends up heading the wrong way 65% of the time.

Why the wobble is bigger than real life

A real knuckleball moves about 20 cm. Here it moves 35–60 cm so it is visible on a phone. The physics papers behind this: APS 2012 and ScienceDaily 2012 (links at the bottom).

Power shot, with a green timing circle test match only
Asked for

Wind up, then smash it, like a green-timed FIFA finish, up to 1.5× power. A slow tap means a less accurate shot.

How

In /star-match-dev, tap ⚡ before aiming, then drag as normal. A 0.45 s wind-up follows, and defenders and the keeper keep moving. Then a green circle pops up on a random part of the ball. Tap it within 0.22 s for 1.5× pace and a dead-straight shot. A slower tap loses the bonus and drifts up to 8.5° off your aim.

Keeper howlers that grow with shot power test match only
Asked for

Spills and shots squirming through the keeper should rise with power. A perfect max-power shot: 0.5–1% for top keepers, 2–3% for average, 5–10% for the worst.

Fix

Any shot the keeper gets to can now go wrong, with a HOWLER! banner. About 2 in 3 squirm through into the net and the rest spill loose in front of goal. A knuckleball almost doubles the chance (× 1.8).

Real football data behind it
  • Opta, Premier League "errors leading to goals" since 2013/14: most are Begovic 18, Lloris 17 and Mignolet 14. That is under 2 a season.
  • Worst single seasons: 5 (Leno, 2018/19; Foderingham, 2023/24).
  • No source was found for shots on target faced per season. With an estimate of 100–150, that is about 0.5% (best keepers) to 3–4% (worst) of all shots. Rates for the hardest shots should sit higher, which matches the table.
  • Sources: 90min · PlanetFootball · Stadium Astro on Opta's definition · knuckleball physics: APS, ScienceDaily. Read from search summaries; the pages themselves were blocked.

Decisions for you

  • 1. Let the match pitch get a little shorter on short phones so it always fits?
    Then the match never scrolls. The pitch would be slightly wider than its normal shape on those phones only. This changes how it looks, so it gets shown side by side first.
  • 2. Move the knuckleball, power shot and howlers into the real game?
    They only exist in the test match (/star-match-dev) for now, as agreed. The move needs Mikey's go-ahead, because it means the core engine file (sensitive).
  • 3. Power shot: always a laced drive?
    Right now you can't curl or chip it, which is why it always hits the wall on free kicks. Keep it, or let it curl?
  • 4. Should a hard ordinary shot also cut the keeper's reach?
    Only power shots do now, so nothing else in the test match changes.
  • 5. Dribble passes skip the even highlight mix
    The chance after a dribble now depends on how far you got, not on the even mix. Harry should say if that's OK.
  • 6. Long shots went up the most (27% → 34% against an average keeper)
    Is that too much? Harry's long-shot setting can pull it back.
