Fixed

  • The aim reticle wasn't actually where your cursor was
    Reported directly on PC — "very offset and not at all on my cursor." A real, provable bug, not a feel issue.
    Cursor round-trip error
    ~0m 0.42m
    How it was actually proven, not just reasoned

    The camera's small downward tilt (from last round's reference-matching push-in) broke the maths that turns a screen point back into an aim target — the inversion silently assumed a dead-level camera. Worked through by hand, then a real script computed both the true projection and its inverse across 500 random targets: with the tilt, aiming at a genuine low corner inverted back roughly 0.42m away from where it actually was — about a fifth of the goal's own playable height, on every single aim.

    Fixed by dropping the tilt to exactly 0, which makes the inversion provably exact again — re-run the same 500-target check afterward: max error ~1e-16m, floating-point noise, not an approximation.

Added

  • A new shot: the first-time strike
    A firm, rushed strike off an already-moving ball — a flatter arc and a shorter reaction window than a shaped drive, its own distinct picture.
  • Near post and far post are now a real, independent split
    Sometimes he's cutting back to finish on his own side; sometimes he's shooting across his own body to the far corner — not always the same shape.
    Measured, not assumed

    222 near-post finishes vs 168 far-post, out of 390 eligible drives in a real 4,000-shot batch — both genuinely common, neither crowding the other out.

  • A real name on screen for every shot
    HEADER FROM A CROSS · VOLLEY · FIRST-TIME STRIKE · CURLING EFFORT · LONG RANGE · NEAR POST · FAR POST — the variety is something you consciously see now, not just something the physics knows about.

Changed

  • The camera doesn't zoom any more — one fixed, wider shot
    Reported directly: "the weird zooming in thing sucks." The three-stage push-in/reveal from last round is gone — the goal, both posts and your own full reach are on screen for the whole sequence, never something that only resolves once a camera move finishes.
    Goal on screen
    96.9% 94.2%
    The same fix that closed the cursor bug

    Dropping the camera's tilt to 0 wasn't just the cursor fix — it also made a tighter, still-safe framing possible: CAM_Y=6.8, down from 7.0, checked corner by corner against the real projection maths so a full-stretch dive into either top corner never clips.

Known issues

Carried forward — still open

Six security holes anyone with the public key can still reach: writing their own XP/rewards, deleting every user's progression, wiping community votes. Two SQL files, both written, both still unrun.

  • Not confirmed live this round unverified
    A real attempt was made — dev server up, a driver built to click through onboarding into Goalie Mode — but the trial-penalty skip sequence proved fragile in headless replay and was cut off rather than keep burning time on an unrelated harness issue.
    What's worth a direct check
    • Does the reticle now sit exactly under the cursor/finger — not just provably correct on paper.
    • Does the goal read as fully visible the whole time, no waiting on a camera cut.
    • Does each shot type (header, volley, first-time, long range, near/far post) actually feel like a different moment.
