# What the original does, round 1 (every frame read, 7 Nov 2026)

Source: R22 "Avocado vs 1000 cuts", frames 0-167 (30 fps).

## Cut frames (the counter goes up on these frames)
- Down: 4, 11, 18, 26 (one every 7-8 frames). Order: right (narrow end) to left.
- Sideways: 62, 72, 82 (one every 10). Order: bottom to top.
- Dice: 124, 139, 154 (one every 15). Order: front to back. The last one drops everything.
- Round 2 starts with a hard cut at frame 166, the knife already resting on the new avocado.

## Knife
- Down: the blade lies IN the cut plane, edge down, tip toward the front. It drops
  through in about 2 frames (blurred), the line appears on that frame, then it rises
  and moves to the next line over about 5 frames.
- Down to sideways (frames 31-59): it swings flat and settles in front of the
  avocado at board level.
- Sideways: flat, handle on the left, it sweeps front to back through the avocado
  in about 2 frames, then comes back to the front at the next height.
- Sideways to dice (frames 85-121): it lies flat in front (85-99), rises and turns
  upright (100-113), and hovers over the front (114-121).
- Dice: upright, edge down, handle on the left; it chops in about 2 frames.

## The avocado and the pieces
- Turned about 20 degrees so the narrow end comes toward the camera; that is why
  the down-cut knife looks thin and diagonal.
- Lines: thin and pale, they appear only when the blade passes.
- After a dice chop the rest has a flat cut face: pale flesh, a darker green rim,
  the stone as a red-brown ring, and faint lines from the earlier cuts.
- Pieces are plain CUBES, each coloured by where it came from: skin green where
  the cube stuck out past the skin, a green rim band, pale flesh, stone brown.
- The slice drops on the chop frame; the cubes tumble forward, bounce a little
  and settle in about 8 frames.
